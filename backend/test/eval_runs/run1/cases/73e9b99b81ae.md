## Query

Consider Eskom's Confidentiality and Non-Disclosure Agreement; Does the document allow the Receiving Party to acquire information similar to the Confidential Information from a third party?

## Gold

The obligations of the Receiving Party pursuant to the provisions of this Agreement shall not apply to any Confidential Information that:
11.6. is received from a Third Party in circumstances that do not result in a breach of the provisions of this Agreement.

## RAG Answer

Yes, but only under the agreement’s exclusions.

Under clause 11.6, the Receiving Party’s confidentiality obligations do **not** apply to information that is **received from a Third Party in circumstances that do not result in a breach of the Agreement**. This means the Receiving Party may obtain similar information from a third party, provided it does so without using, disclosing, or otherwise exploiting the Disclosing Party’s Confidential Information in violation of the NDA.

However, if the third-party acquisition involves a breach of the Agreement—for example, misuse of Eskom’s Confidential Information or inducing another party to breach—the exclusion would not apply.
