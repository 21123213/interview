## Query

Consider the Non-Disclosure Agreement between CopAcc and ToP Mentors; Does the document restrict the use of Confidential Information to the purposes stated in the Agreement?

## Gold

Mentor shall not use any Confidential Information for any purpose except to review, assess and help develop the Participants´ Ideas.

## RAG Answer

Yes. The Non-Disclosure Agreement restricts the use of Confidential Information to the purposes stated in the Agreement.

Specifically, under Clause 5, the Mentor “shall not use any Confidential Information for any purpose except to review, assess and help develop the Participants’ Ideas.” The same clause also states that the Mentor shall not use Confidential Information for the Mentor’s own purposes or for third parties’ purposes.

Clause 8 further confirms this restriction by providing that the Mentor has no right, license, title, or interest in Confidential Information, except the limited right to review, assess, and help develop the Confidential Information in connection with the Copernicus Accelerator 2017.
