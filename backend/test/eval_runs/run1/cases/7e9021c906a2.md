## Query

Consider FNHA's Non-Disclosure Agreement; Does the document permit the Receiving Party to create a copy of some Confidential Information under certain circumstances?

## Gold

Company will not make copies of, disclose, discuss, publish or disseminate Confidential Information any third person or entity.

## RAG Answer

No. Based on the provided FNHA Non-Disclosure Agreement, the Receiving Party (“Company”) is expressly prohibited from making copies of Confidential Information: “Company will not make copies of, disclose, discuss, publish or disseminate Confidential Information any third person or entity.”

The document only contains a limited exception for discussions of Confidential Information with FNHA staff or other persons if expressly permitted by FNHA and conducted under FNHA-directed terms and conditions. It does not state that copies may be created under those circumstances.
