## Query

Consider FNHA's Non-Disclosure Agreement; Does the document allow the Receiving Party to share some Confidential Information with their employees?

## Gold

This Agreement does not preclude discussions of Confidential Information between the undersigned and FNHA staff or with any other persons identified by FNHA as having undertaken this Agreement.

## RAG Answer

Based on the FNHA NDA, the Receiving Party is **not given a general right** to share Confidential Information with its employees.

The document prohibits disclosure to “any third person or entity,” but it does not preclude discussions of Confidential Information:

- between the undersigned and FNHA staff;
- with other persons **identified by FNHA** as having undertaken the Agreement; or
- with other persons **if expressly permitted by FNHA staff** and conducted on terms FNHA may direct.

Therefore, the Receiving Party may share Confidential Information with employees **only if those employees are identified by FNHA as having undertaken the NDA or are expressly permitted by FNHA staff under FNHA’s terms**. Otherwise, sharing with employees is not allowed.
