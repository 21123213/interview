## Query

Consider the Non-Disclosure Agreement between DoiT and ICN; Does the document state that Confidential Information shall only include technical information?

## Gold

WHEREAS, both parties, for their mutual benefit, desire to have the State disclose to Vendor certain specifications, designs, plans, drawings or other business and/or technical information which is proprietary, restricted or secret, and confidential to the State (“CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION").

## RAG Answer

No. The document states that “CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION” includes “certain specifications, designs, plans, drawings or other business and/or technical information which is proprietary, restricted or secret, and confidential,” so it is not limited only to technical information.
