## Query

Consider the Non-Disclosure Agreement between CopAcc and ToP Mentors; Does the document allow the Receiving Party to acquire information similar to the Confidential Information from a third party?

## Gold

Confidential Information does not include:
> information received from a third party who was free to disclose such information.

## RAG Answer

Yes. The NDA states that “Confidential Information” does not include “information received from a third party who was free to disclose such information.”  

Therefore, if the Receiving Party (the Mentor) obtains information from a third party who had the right to disclose it, that information is not treated as Confidential Information under the agreement. However, the document does not expressly address information merely “similar” to Confidential Information; it only excludes third-party information if the third party was free to disclose it.
