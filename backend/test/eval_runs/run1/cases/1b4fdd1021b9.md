## Query

Consider the Data Use Agreement in New York City; Does the document indicate that the Agreement does not grant the Receiving Party any rights to the Confidential Information?

## Gold

The Data Recipient hereby acknowledges that the DOHMH is the exclusive owner of the Data and all trade secrets and other rights therein. No license or conveyance of any such rights is granted or implied under this Agreement.

## RAG Answer

Yes, in substance—but with a terminology caveat.

The provided excerpts do not use the exact phrases “Receiving Party” or “Confidential Information.” They instead refer to the “Data Recipient” and the “Data.” However, the Agreement clearly indicates that the Data Recipient is not granted ownership or general rights in the Data.

Specifically, Section III.C states:

- The DOHMH is the “exclusive owner of the Data and all trade secrets and other rights therein.”
- “No license or conveyance of any such rights is granted or implied under this Agreement.”
- The Data Recipient may use the Data only for the purposes stated in Attachment B.
- Upon DOHMH request or termination, the Data Recipient must return or destroy the Data and all copies.

So the Agreement does not grant the Data Recipient broad rights to the confidential Data. It only permits limited access and use for approved purposes under strict confidentiality and security conditions.
