## Query

Consider Excelerate's Non-Disclosure Agreement; Does the document allow the Receiving Party to acquire information similar to the Confidential Information from a third party?

## Gold

6. The conditions of Paragraph 4 hereof shall not apply to information which:
b. Was
(ii) is later received by the receiving Party from a third party, unless the receiving Party knows or has reason to know of an obligation of secrecy of the third party to the disclosing Party with respect to such information; or

## RAG Answer

Yes. Under Excelerate’s NDA, the confidentiality obligations in Paragraph 4 do **not** apply to information that the Receiving Party later receives from a third party, **unless** the Receiving Party knows or has reason to know that the third party has an obligation of secrecy to the disclosing Party.

In other words, the Receiving Party may acquire similar/proprietary information from a third party without being bound by the NDA’s confidentiality restrictions, provided the third party is not under a secrecy obligation to the disclosing Party.
