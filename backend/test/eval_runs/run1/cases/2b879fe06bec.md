## Query

Consider Excelerate's Non-Disclosure Agreement; Does the document state that Confidential Information shall only include technical information?

## Gold

Each Party's Proprietary Information may include, but is not limited to, patents, copyrights, design methods, ideas, concepts, data, formulas, manufacturing techniques, know-how, business plans, customer lists, solicitation response strategies, technical solutions to client requirements, system architectures, proposal preparation techniques and pricing policies, software, methodologies, technologies, processes, financial information, and sales and marketing information.

## RAG Answer

No. The document does not state that confidential/proprietary information is limited to technical information.

Under Paragraph 1 of the Excelerate Non-Disclosure Agreement, “Proprietary Information” means proprietary or confidential information owned or controlled by a party and may include, but is not limited to, patents, copyrights, design methods, ideas, concepts, data, formulas, manufacturing techniques, know-how, business plans, customer lists, solicitation response strategies, technical solutions, system architectures, proposal preparation techniques, pricing policies, software, methodologies, technologies, processes, financial information, and sales and marketing information.

Therefore, the definition is broader than technical information alone.
