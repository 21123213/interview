## Query

Consider Excelerate's Non-Disclosure Agreement; Does the document allow verbally conveyed information to be considered as Confidential Information?

## Gold

5. Disclosure of Proprietary Information to a receiving Party may be either oral or in writing. If an oral disclosure occurs, it will be confirmed within fifteen (15) days following initial disclosure by a written communication stating at least the date and circumstances under which the disclosure occurred and the general nature of the information disclosed.

## RAG Answer

Yes. Excelerate’s NDA allows orally conveyed information to be treated as confidential/proprietary information.

Under Paragraph 5, disclosure of Proprietary Information “may be either oral or in writing.” However, if the disclosure is oral, it must be confirmed within fifteen (15) days by a written communication stating at least:

- the date of disclosure,
- the circumstances under which it occurred, and
- the general nature of the information disclosed.

So verbal information can be confidential, but only if properly confirmed in writing within the required 15-day period.
