## Query

Consider APIC's Confidentiality Agreement; Does the document state that Confidential Information shall only include technical information?

## Gold

a) To hold in confidence any and all technical or business information about the company’s product which is disclosed, or made available to you directly or indirectly, or is information you otherwise receive incident to your participation in this discussion;

## RAG Answer

No. The APIC/Focus Group Confidentiality Agreement does not limit Confidential Information to technical information only.

It states that Confidential Information includes the products themselves and the ideas and concepts regarding manufacture, marketing, use, and discussions. It also expressly covers “any and all technical or business information,” as well as personally identifiable information (“PII”) of participants.
