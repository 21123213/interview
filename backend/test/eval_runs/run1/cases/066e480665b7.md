## Query

Consider Excelerate's Non-Disclosure Agreement; Does the document mention that some obligations of the Agreement may survive the termination of the Agreement?

## Gold

a. This Agreement shall terminate at the end of the period of years as provided for in paragraph 4 above, or upon the delivery of written notice of termination by a Party to the other Party; however, the obligations of a receiving Party pursuant to Paragraph 4 shall remain in effect for the term specified therein.
b. Notwithstanding the termination of this agreement, the supplied data must be maintained and protected in accordance with its provisions for three (3) years following the termination of this agreement.

## RAG Answer

Yes. Excelerate’s Non-Disclosure Agreement states that some obligations survive termination. Specifically, Section 10 provides that upon termination, the receiving party’s obligations under Paragraph 4 remain in effect for the specified term, and that notwithstanding termination, supplied data must be maintained and protected for three (3) years following termination. It also requires proprietary data to be returned or destroyed at the conclusion of the agreement, except for one copy retained by legal counsel.
