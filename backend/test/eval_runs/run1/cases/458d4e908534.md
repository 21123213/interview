## Query

Consider EFCA's Non-Disclosure Agreement; Does the document allow the Receiving Party to share some Confidential Information with third parties, including consultants, agents, and professional advisors?

## Gold

EFCA shall ensure that disclosure of such Confidential Information is restricted to those employees or directors of EFCA and EFCA’s principals having the need to know the same.
4.1 EFCA shall:
4.1.1 not divulge the disclosing party’s Confidential Information, in whole or in part, to any third party; and

## RAG Answer

No. Under the EFCA Non-Disclosure Agreement, the Receiving Party (EFCA) is generally prohibited from sharing Confidential Information with third parties.

- Clause 4.1.1 states that EFCA shall **“not divulge the disclosing party’s Confidential Information, in whole or in part, to any third party.”**
- Clause 2 limits disclosure to **employees, directors, and EFCA’s principals** who have a need to know.
- The document does **not** provide an exception permitting disclosure to **consultants, agents, or professional advisors**.

The only apparent exception is under Clause 4.1.2, which allows EFCA to make a disclosure **required by law**, provided it gives the disclosing party at least two business days’ notice.
