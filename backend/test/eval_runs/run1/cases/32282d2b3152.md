## Query

Consider EFCA's Non-Disclosure Agreement; Does the document permit the Receiving Party to retain some Confidential Information even after its return or destruction?

## Gold

3.1 EFCA shall:
3.1.1 immediately upon receipt of a written request from the disclosing part, return to the disclosing party all documents and materials (and all copies thereof) containing the disclosing party’s Confidential Information and certify in writing to the disclosing party that it has complied with the requirements of this sub-clause; and

## RAG Answer

No. Based on the available EFCA confidentiality agreement text, the Receiving Party (EFCA) is required to return **all documents and materials containing the disclosing party’s Confidential Information, including all copies**, upon written request (Clause 3.1.1). The document does not include an express provision permitting EFCA to retain any Confidential Information after return or destruction.

Clause 3.1.2 only states that EFCA remains bound by confidentiality obligations after the purpose is completed or the materials are returned; it does not authorize retention.
