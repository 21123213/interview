## Query

Consider DBT's Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement; Does the document include a clause that prevents the Receiving Party from disclosing the fact that the Agreement was agreed upon or negotiated?

## Gold

The existence of any business negotiations, discussions, consultations, or agreements in progress between the parties shall not be released to any form of public media, unless agreed between the parties in writing.

## RAG Answer

Yes. The provided DBT Mutual NDA includes a related clause in Section 2 (“Obligations”):

> “The existence of any business negotiations, discussions, consultations, or agreements in progress between the parties shall not be released to any form of public media, unless agreed between the parties in writing.”

However, the clause is limited: it prohibits releasing the existence of negotiations or “agreements in progress” to public media unless the parties agree in writing. It does not expressly prohibit all disclosure of the fact that the Agreement was agreed upon or negotiated outside public media.
