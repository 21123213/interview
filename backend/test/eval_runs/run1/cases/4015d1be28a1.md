## Query

Consider EFCA's Non-Disclosure Agreement; Does the document require the Receiving Party to notify the Disclosing Party if they are required by law, regulation, or judicial process to disclose any Confidential Information?

## Gold

Notwithstanding the foregoing, EFCA shall be entitled to make any disclosure required by law of the disclosing party’s Confidential Information provided that it gives the disclosing party not less than two business days’ notice of such disclosure.

## RAG Answer

Yes. Under clause 4.1.2, EFCA may disclose the disclosing party’s Confidential Information if such disclosure is required by law, provided it gives the disclosing party not less than two business days’ notice of the disclosure.

However, the excerpt does not expressly mention “regulation” or “judicial process”; it only refers to disclosure “required by law.”
