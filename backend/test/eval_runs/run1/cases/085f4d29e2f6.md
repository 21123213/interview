## RAG Answer

Based on the provided excerpts, **the document does not expressly restrict the use of Confidential Information solely to the purposes stated in the Agreement**.

It does impose related restrictions, including:

- keeping Confidential Information confidential;
- limiting disclosure to EFCA employees, directors, and principals with a need to know;
- prohibiting commercial use without the disclosing party’s prior written consent; and
- continuing confidentiality obligations even after completion of any purpose for which information was disclosed.

However, the available text does not contain a clear clause stating that Confidential Information may only be used for the Agreement’s stated purpose.
