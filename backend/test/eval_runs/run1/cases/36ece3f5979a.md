## Query

Consider the Non-Disclosure Agreement between CopAcc and ToP Mentors; Does the document permit the Receiving Party to retain some Confidential Information even after its return or destruction?

## Gold

At Organiser’s first request, Mentor shall:
(d) erase and/or destroy any Confidential Information contained in computer memory or data storage apparatus of, under control of or used by Mentor;
(e) remove the Confidential Information from any software or data base of, under control of/or used by Mentor that incorporates or uses the Confidential Information in whole or in part; and

## RAG Answer

No.

Based on the provided NDA excerpt, the Receiving Party (the Mentor) is not expressly permitted to retain Confidential Information after return or destruction.

Clause 6 requires the Mentor, at the Organiser’s first request, to:

- discontinue all use of Confidential Information;
- return all materials containing Confidential Information;
- destroy any copies and materials containing Confidential Information;
- erase or destroy Confidential Information in computer memory or data storage;
- remove Confidential Information from any software or database; and
- warrant in writing that all such actions have been taken.

There is no clause in the provided text allowing the Mentor to keep an archival copy or otherwise retain Confidential Information after return/destruction. The only related exception is that information falling outside the definition of “Confidential Information” under Clause 4 — for example, information already known, independently developed, publicly available without wrongdoing, or received from a third party free to disclose it — is not subject to the confidentiality obligations.
