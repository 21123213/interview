## RAG Answer

Yes. Under the HNBA Confidentiality Agreement, if the Board Member/Receiving Party is legally required to disclose Confidential Information, the Agreement requires the Board Member to provide written notice to the HNBA and/or the other disclosing party within five (5) days after learning of that legal requirement, so the disclosing party can pursue its rights regarding the potential disclosure.
