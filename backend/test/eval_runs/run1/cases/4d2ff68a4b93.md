## Query

## RAG Answer

No — based on the provided document, there is no general permission for the Receiving Party to share Confidential Information with third parties such as consultants, agents, or professional advisors.

The NDA states that the contracting parties must not make Confidential Information available to third parties without prior consent. It only provides limited exceptions:

- The university may reveal Confidential Information to third parties if necessary for the proper performance of the assessment process.
- The Receiving Party may disclose information if it has a legal or official duty to do so.

Therefore, sharing with consultants, agents, or professional advisors is not expressly allowed unless it falls within one of these narrow exceptions or prior consent is obtained.
