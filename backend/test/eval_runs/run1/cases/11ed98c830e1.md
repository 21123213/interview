## Query

Consider the Non-Disclosure Agreement between CopAcc and ToP Mentors; Does the document allow the Receiving Party to share some Confidential Information with their employees?

## Gold

Mentor shall not disclose any Confidential Information to any third party or to Mentor’s employees and/or employer without the prior written consent of the Participants.

## RAG Answer

Yes, but only conditionally.

Under Clause 5, the Mentor/Receiving Party **may not disclose Confidential Information to its employees or employer without the prior written consent of the Participants**. If such consent is obtained and employees will have access to the Confidential Information, the Mentor must require those employees to sign a non-disclosure agreement that protects the Confidential Information **to at least the same degree** as the main NDA.
