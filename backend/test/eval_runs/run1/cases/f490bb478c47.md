## Query

Consider Grindrod SA's Non-Disclosure Agreement; Does the document allow the Receiving Party to share some Confidential Information with their employees?

## Gold

3. For the purposes of this Confidentiality and Non-Disclosure Undertaking, The Vendor undertakes;
3.3. not to disclose such Confidential Information to any person without the prior written authorization from Grindrod SA,

## RAG Answer

No — the document does not expressly allow the Receiving Party (the Vendor) to share Confidential Information with its employees.

It states that the Vendor undertakes:

- to hold Confidential Information in strict confidence; and  
- **not to disclose such Confidential Information to any person without the prior written authorization from Grindrod SA**.

There is no explicit exception permitting disclosure to the Vendor’s own employees. The document only requires the Vendor to ensure that all persons given access to Confidential Information complete and forward a copy of the undertaking to Grindrod SA’s Head of Procurement, but that does not itself create permission to disclose without written authorization.
