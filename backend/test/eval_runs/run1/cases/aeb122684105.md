## Query

Consider Grindrod SA's Non-Disclosure Agreement; Does the document allow the Receiving Party to independently develop information that is similar to the Confidential Information?

## Gold

Confidential Information also excludes information in the public domain for a reason, other than a breach of this Confidentiality and Non-Disclosure Undertaking, with any party, or independently developed by the Vendor without reference to information provided by, Grindrod SA;

## RAG Answer

Yes—based on the provided document, the Vendor (the Receiving Party) may independently develop information that is similar to Grindrod SA’s Confidential Information, provided it is developed **without reference to information provided by Grindrod SA**.

The document expressly excludes from “Confidential Information” any information “independently developed by the Vendor without reference to information provided by Grindrod SA.” Therefore, independently developed similar information is not treated as Confidential Information. However, if the Vendor develops the information using or with reference to Grindrod SA’s Confidential Information, it would remain subject to the confidentiality and use restrictions in the agreement.
