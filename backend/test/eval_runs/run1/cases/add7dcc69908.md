## Query

Consider Evelozcity's Non-Disclosure Agreement; Does the document restrict the use of Confidential Information to the purposes stated in the Agreement?

## Gold

Recipient will, and will cause its Representatives to
(ii) use the Confidential Information of the Disclosing Party for no purpose other than the Permitted Use.

## RAG Answer

Yes. Section 2 of the Evelozcity Non-Disclosure Agreement defines a “Permitted Use” as use of the Confidential Information for evaluating or pursuing a potential business relationship with the Disclosing Party or its affiliates. It also states that the Recipient and its Representatives shall use the Confidential Information “for no purpose other than the Permitted Use.” Therefore, the document restricts the use of Confidential Information to the purposes stated in the Agreement, subject to the limited exceptions in Section 3.
