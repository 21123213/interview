## Query

Consider the Non-Disclosure Agreement between Hochschule Furtwangen University and Thesis Participants; Does the document state that Confidential Information shall only include technical information?

## Gold

## RAG Answer

No. The document does not state that Confidential Information shall only include technical information.

Under § 2, Confidential Information is defined more broadly as information that is:

- protected as company or business secrets under § 3 b LwVfG BW, or  
- subject to banking confidentiality, data protection, or any other legal obligation to confidentiality.

Therefore, the definition is not limited to technical information.
