## Query

Consider EFCA's Non-Disclosure Agreement; Does the document include a clause that prevents the Receiving Party from disclosing the fact that the Agreement was agreed upon or negotiated?

## Gold

EFCA agrees to keep the existence and nature of this Agreement confidential and not to use the same or the name of the disclosing party (or of any company in the Group of Companies of which the disclosing party forms part) in any publicity, advertisement or other disclosure with regard to this Agreement without the prior written consent of the disclosing party.

## RAG Answer

Yes.

Clause 5 (“Confidentiality”) states that EFCA, as the Receiving Party, agrees to keep the existence and nature of the Agreement confidential. It also prohibits EFCA from using the disclosing party’s name in any publicity, advertisement, or other disclosure regarding the Agreement without the disclosing party’s prior written consent.

This effectively prevents the Receiving Party from disclosing the fact that the Agreement was agreed upon or negotiated, unless the disclosing party gives prior written consent.
