## Query

Consider the Data Use Agreement in New York City; Does the document mention that some obligations of the Agreement may survive the termination of the Agreement?

## Gold

2. Upon the termination of this Agreement for any reason, the confidentiality provisions set forth herein shall continue to apply to the Data shared with Data Recipient pursuant to this Agreement.
Upon the Expiration of this Agreement, only the continued use of Data for the purposes set forth in Attachment B will cease. All other provisions of this Agreement, including this Section V, shall survive.

## RAG Answer

Yes. The document states that certain obligations continue after termination.

- Under **Effect of Termination**, it says that upon termination for any reason, the **confidentiality provisions continue to apply** to the Data shared, and the Data Recipient must **return or destroy the Data** within 60 days, or, if return/destruction is infeasible, must continue to protect the Data.
- It also includes a **Survival** provision stating that upon expiration, only continued use of the Data for Attachment B purposes ceases, and **all other provisions of the Agreement survive**.
