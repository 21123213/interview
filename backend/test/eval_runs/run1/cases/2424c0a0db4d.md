## Query

Consider the Data Use Agreement in New York City; Does the document restrict the use of Confidential Information to the purposes stated in the Agreement?

## Gold

A. Data Recipient agrees to use the Data solely for the purposes set forth in Attachment B to this Agreement, and for no other purposes.
Except as otherwise provided in this Agreement, Data Recipient shall not, at any time, directly or indirectly disclose, share, give, loan, sell, or otherwise grant access to the Data provided pursuant to this Agreement, in part or in whole, to any other person or organization.

## RAG Answer

Yes. The Data Use Agreement restricts the use of the confidential Data to the purposes stated in the Agreement.

Specifically, Section IV states that the Data Recipient “agrees to use the Data solely for the purposes set forth in Attachment B to this Agreement, and for no other purposes.” Section II also confirms that Attachment B describes the permitted use of the Data. In addition, Section III provides that the Data Recipient may not use, incorporate, or derive products or items from the Data “other than for the purpose stated in Attachment B.” The Agreement further requires the Data Recipient to maintain the confidentiality and security of the Data and prohibits unauthorized disclosure or use.
