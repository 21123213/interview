## Query

Consider Euler Hermes's Non-Disclosure Agreement; Does the document mention that some obligations of the Agreement may survive the termination of the Agreement?

## Gold

Upon the Company’s written request, Euler Hermes shall promptly return to the Company or destroy the Confidential Information in its possession but may retain copies of any and all notes, analyses, references, or other material prepared by Euler Hermes that incorporates any of the Confidential Information, which shall remain subject to the confidentiality obligations of this Agreement, nothwithstanding Paragraph 10 of this Agreement.

## RAG Answer

Yes, but only in a limited way.

Paragraph 4 states that if Euler Hermes retains copies of notes, analyses, references, or other materials incorporating the Confidential Information, those materials “shall remain subject to the confidentiality obligations of this Agreement, notwithstanding Paragraph 10.” Paragraph 10 provides that the Agreement expires after five years.

This indicates that confidentiality obligations may continue after the Agreement’s expiration with respect to retained materials. However, the document does not contain a general survival clause stating that other obligations survive termination.
