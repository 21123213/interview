## Query

Consider the Non-Disclosure Agreement between DoiT and ICN; Does the document indicate that the Agreement does not grant the Receiving Party any rights to the Confidential Information?

## Gold

2. No license under any trademark, patent, copyright, mask work protection right or any other intellectual property right is either granted or implied by conveying CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION to Vendor.

## RAG Answer

Yes, in part. The Agreement states in Section 2 that:

> “No license under any trademark, patent, copyright, mask work protection right or any other intellectual property right is either granted or implied by conveying CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION to Vendor.”

This indicates that the Receiving Party (the Vendor) is not granted any intellectual-property rights in the Confidential Information merely by receiving it.

However, Section 1 does grant a limited right to use the Confidential Information “only for the purpose of discussing street level route information for the State’s fiber optic network data points and paths.” So the Agreement does not grant IP rights, but it does allow a narrow use right for the stated purpose.
