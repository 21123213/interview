## Query

Consider DBT's Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement; Does the document require the Receiving Party to notify the Disclosing Party if they are required by law, regulation, or judicial process to disclose any Confidential Information?

## Gold

Confidential Information shall not include any information that:
(v) the Confidential Information is required to be disclosed pursuant to a requirement of a governmental agency or law so long as the other party is provided notice of such requirement prior to any such disclosure.

## RAG Answer

Yes. Under the provided DBT Mutual NDA excerpt, Section 1 states that Confidential Information does not include information that is “required to be disclosed pursuant to a requirement of a governmental agency or law so long as the other party is provided notice of such requirement prior to any such disclosure.”

Therefore, if the Receiving Party is required to disclose Confidential Information by a governmental agency or law, it must notify the other party before making that disclosure.

However, the excerpt does not expressly mention “regulation” or “judicial process,” so the answer is limited to the language provided.
