## Query

Consider the Data Use Agreement in New York City; Does the document allow the Receiving Party to share some Confidential Information with third parties, including consultants, agents, and professional advisors?

## Gold

1. Only the Data Recipient’s employees and/or consultants required to use the Data to perform the functions of this Agreement that are set forth in Attachment B, and so designated by Data Recipient as “Authorized Users” in Attachment C to this Agreement, will be given access to the Data.
A. Nothing express or implied in this Agreement is intended to confer, nor shall anything herein confer, upon any person other than the Parties, any rights, remedies, obligations, or liabilities whatsoever.

## RAG Answer

Yes, but only in a limited way. The agreement does not give the Receiving Party (the “Data Recipient”) a general right to share Confidential Information/Data with third parties such as agents or professional advisors.

It permits access only to the Data Recipient’s **employees and/or consultants** who are:

- required to use the Data to perform the approved functions under Attachment B;
- designated as “Authorized Users” in Attachment C;
- trained on confidentiality and security; and
- bound by an Authorized User Agreement approved by DOHMH.

The agreement also generally prohibits the Data Recipient from disclosing, sharing, or granting access to the Data to “any other person or organization,” except as otherwise provided in the agreement.

Therefore, sharing with consultants may be allowed if they are properly designated Authorized Users. Sharing with agents or professional advisors is not expressly permitted by the provided text unless they also qualify as Authorized Users and the agreement’s confidentiality, security, and consent requirements are satisfied.
