## Query

Consider the Tabun Kitchen Investments' Non-Disclosure Agreement; Does the document indicate that the Agreement does not grant the Receiving Party any rights to the Confidential Information?

## Gold

All materials and Confidential Information shall remain the exclusive property of the Disclosing Party. Except as expressly provided herein or under a separate written agreement between the parties that references this Agreement, either party shall not be obligated to grant, convey or transfer to the other any interest, license or other right, or under its Confidential Information or any patent, copyright, trade secret, trademark or other intellectual property right.

## RAG Answer

Yes. The Agreement states in Clause 5 (“Ownership; No License”) that all materials and Confidential Information remain the exclusive property of the Disclosing Party, and that, except as expressly provided in the Agreement or under a separate written agreement referencing it, neither party is obligated to grant, convey, or transfer any interest, license, or other right in the Confidential Information or related intellectual property.
