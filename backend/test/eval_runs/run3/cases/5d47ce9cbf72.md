## Query

Consider the Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement between Mutual Customer and Allazo Electronics; Does the document require the Receiving Party to notify the Disclosing Party if they are required by law, regulation, or judicial process to disclose any Confidential Information?

## Gold

Nothing contained in this Agreement shall restrict the Receiving Party from disclosing Confidential Information that is required to be disclosed under any law, subpoena or court order provided that the Receiving Party provides the Disclosing Party with prompt notice so that the Disclosing Party may, at its expense, seek a protective order or take other appropriate measures.
In the event a Court Order is issued to either party, the party complying with such order shall send notice to the other unless specifically prohibited to do so by the court order.

## RAG Answer

Yes. Under Section 7 (“Court Orders”), if the Receiving Party is required to disclose Confidential Information under any law, subpoena, or court order, it may do so only if it provides the Disclosing Party with prompt notice, allowing the Disclosing Party to seek a protective order or take other appropriate measures.

Additionally, if a court order is issued to either party, the party complying with the order must send notice to the other party unless specifically prohibited from doing so by the court order.
