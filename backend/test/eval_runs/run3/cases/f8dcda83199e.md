## Query

Consider Sony Pictures Television's Non-Disclosure Agreement; Does the document permit the Receiving Party to create a copy of some Confidential Information under certain circumstances?

## Gold

Each party, as a Receiving Party, agrees that it will:
(d) not copy or reproduce all or any part of such Confidential Information in any medium, except as may be strictly necessary to facilitate the use of Confidential Information as permitted by this Agreement; and

## RAG Answer

Yes. The agreement generally prohibits the Receiving Party from copying Confidential Information, but permits copying in limited circumstances.

Under Section 3(d), the Receiving Party may not copy or reproduce all or any part of the Confidential Information “except as may be strictly necessary to facilitate the use of Confidential Information as permitted by this Agreement.”

In addition, Section 12 permits the parties and their Representatives to retain Confidential Information where required by legal or regulatory requirements, including internal compliance procedures, and allows electronically stored Confidential Information saved in ordinary backup files to remain, provided it continues to be protected under the agreement.
