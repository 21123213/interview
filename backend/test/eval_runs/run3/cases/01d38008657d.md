## Query

Consider the Agreement between SE_NDCA and PRE-QUAL PACKAGE; Does the document allow the Receiving Party to acquire information similar to the Confidential Information from a third party?

## Gold

Confidential Information shall not include:
(b) information which is or becomes available on a non-confidential basis from a source which is not known to the Receiving Party to be prohibited from disclosing such information pursuant to a legal, contractual or fiduciary obligation to the Disclosing Party;

## RAG Answer

Yes, but only to the extent the information is not treated as Confidential Information under the Agreement.

The Agreement excludes from Confidential Information:

- information that is or becomes available on a non-confidential basis from a third-party source, provided the Receiving Party does not know that the source is prohibited from disclosing it due to a legal, contractual, or fiduciary obligation; and  
- information developed by or for the Receiving Party independently of the Disclosing Party’s Confidential Information.

Therefore, the Receiving Party may acquire or use similar information from a third party if it is obtained lawfully and non-confidentially from that source, or independently developed. However, it may not use or disclose information that remains Confidential Information received from the Disclosing Party, or information obtained from a source known to be prohibited from disclosing it.
