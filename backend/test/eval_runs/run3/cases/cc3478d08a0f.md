## Query

Consider RROI's Confidentiality Agreement; Does the document permit the Receiving Party to create a copy of some Confidential Information under certain circumstances?

## Gold

2. Only for Intended Purpose: The Recipient agrees and shall cause its Representatives to agree:
(iv) not copy or reproduce any written materials comprising a part of the Evaluation Material, without the prior written consent of the Disclosing Party.
Furthermore, it is acknowledged that the Recipient’s computer system may automatically back-up Evaluation Material disclosed to it under the Agreement.

## RAG Answer

Yes, but only in limited circumstances.

Under the RROI Confidentiality Agreement, the Recipient/Receiving Party is generally prohibited from copying or reproducing written Evaluation Material without the Disclosing Party’s prior written consent.

However, the document also acknowledges that the Recipient’s computer system may automatically create back-up copies of Evaluation Material. In that situation, the Recipient may retain such automatically created copies in normal archival or back-up computer storage for the period it normally retains such records, provided the copies remain subject to the confidentiality obligations and are not accessed except as required by the Agreement.

So, the Agreement does not generally permit voluntary copying, but it allows copying only if the Disclosing Party gives prior written consent, and it permits retention of certain automatically generated back-up copies under the specified conditions.
