## Query

Consider the Non-Disclosure Agreement between University of Wisconsin Oshkosh; Does the document require that all Confidential Information be expressly identified by the Disclosing Party?

## Gold

1. All information disclosed by one party to the other to evaluate the Project and/or Purpose that is designated in writing as “Confidential” at the time of disclosure or if disclosed orally is designated in writing as “Confidential” within fifteen (15) days of disclosure is “Confidential Information.”
6. Confidential Information will be used only to evaluate the Project and/or Purpose and will be provided in writing and marked "Confidential."

## RAG Answer

Yes. The NDA requires Confidential Information to be expressly identified by the Disclosing Party.

Under Section 1, information is only “Confidential Information” if it is:

- designated in writing as “Confidential” at the time of disclosure, if disclosed in writing; or  
- designated in writing as “Confidential” within 15 days of disclosure, if disclosed orally.

Section 6 also states that Confidential Information will be provided in writing and marked “Confidential.”

Therefore, information that is not expressly marked or designated as “Confidential” by the Disclosing Party is not treated as Confidential Information under the agreement.
