## Query

Consider Simply Fashion's Non-Disclosure Agreement; Does the document allow the Receiving Party to share some Confidential Information with third parties, including consultants, agents, and professional advisors?

## Gold

Potential Purchaser may disclose the Evaluation Materials to its parents, subsidiaries, parents’ subsidiaries, or affiliates (collectively, the “Affiliated Entities”) and Potential Purchaser’s and/or the Affiliated Entities’, directors, officers, employees, partners, consultants, potential financing sources, joint-venture partners, bankers, accountants, lenders, investors, insurance consultants and/or brokers, attorneys, agents and financial and legal advisors (collectively, such entities or persons to whom Potential Purchaser or an Affiliated Entity discloses the Evaluation Materials, “Representatives”) who may need access to the Evaluation Materials, in Potential Purchaser’s discretion, for the purpose of evaluating the Proposed Transaction.

## RAG Answer

Yes. Under Simply Fashion’s Confidentiality and Non-Disclosure Agreement, the Receiving Party — referred to in the document as the “Potential Purchaser” — may share certain Confidential Information, called the “Evaluation Materials,” with specified third parties, including consultants, agents, and professional advisors, but only on limited terms.

Section 1 states that the Potential Purchaser may disclose the Evaluation Materials to its Affiliated Entities and to its or its Affiliated Entities’ directors, officers, employees, partners, consultants, potential financing sources, joint-venture partners, bankers, accountants, lenders, investors, insurance consultants and/or brokers, attorneys, agents, and financial and legal advisors, collectively defined as “Representatives,” if those persons need access for the purpose of evaluating the Proposed Transaction.

However, this permission is not a general right to share with third parties. The document requires that the Potential Purchaser:

1. inform in writing all Representatives who receive any portion of the Evaluation Materials that the information is confidential and proprietary and that they are bound by the Agreement; and  
2. direct those Representatives in writing to comply with the confidentiality and non-use terms of the Agreement.

Also, the Agreement generally prohibits disclosure to any third party without the Company’s prior written consent, except as expressly permitted in the Agreement.

So, the document does allow sharing with consultants, agents, and professional advisors, but only as “Representatives” who need access for evaluating the Proposed Transaction and only if they are properly notified of and directed to comply with the confidentiality obligations.
