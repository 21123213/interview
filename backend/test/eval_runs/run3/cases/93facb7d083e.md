## RAG Answer

Yes, but only in a limited way.

The Agreement restricts Petro-Lube’s use of **Samples** to performing the Evaluations directed by Your Company Name (Clause 3.2.3) and limits access to employees who need it for Evaluation purposes (Clause 3.2.4).

However, the provided text does **not** clearly impose a general restriction on the use of all **Confidential Information** for the Agreement’s stated purposes. Clause 4.1 instead states that confidential technical/commercial data need not be disclosed, and if disclosed, may be regarded as non-confidential unless the parties agree otherwise in writing.
