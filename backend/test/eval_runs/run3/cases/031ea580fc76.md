## Query

Consider the Confidentiality Agreement between QEP and BMO; Does the document indicate that the Agreement does not grant the Receiving Party any rights to the Confidential Information?

## Gold

(b) Nothing in this Agreement shall be construed as granting any rights to the Receiving Company or any of its Representatives under any patent, copyright, trademark, trade secret, or other intellectual property right of Owner, nor shall this Agreement be construed to grant to the Receiving Company or any of its Representatives any licenses or other rights in or to the Confidential Information except the limited right to review the Confidential Information solely for the purposes of determining whether to enter into the Transaction. Nothing contained in this Agreement is intended to confer upon the Receiving Company or its Representatives any right whatsoever to any interest Owner has or may have in the Properties.

## RAG Answer

Yes. The document states in Section 5(b) that nothing in the Agreement grants the Receiving Company or its Representatives any licenses or other rights in or to the Confidential Information, except a limited right to review the Confidential Information solely for the purpose of determining whether to enter into the Transaction. Section 6 also confirms that the Confidential Information remains the property of Owner.
