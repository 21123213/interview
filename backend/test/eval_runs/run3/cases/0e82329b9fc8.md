## Query

Consider Oceaneering's Non-Disclosure Agreement; Does the document permit the Receiving Party to retain some Confidential Information even after its return or destruction?

## Gold

8. Upon Oceaneering’s written request, Recipient shall promptly: (i) deliver to Oceaneering and cease to use all Confidential Information in Recipient’s (including its Representatives’) possession, custody or control; or (ii) destroy the same and delete all electronic records containing the Confidential Information, provided that Oceaneering may require Recipient to certify in writing such destruction and deletion.

## RAG Answer

No. Based on the provided document, the Receiving Party is not permitted to retain Confidential Information after Oceaneering’s written request for return or destruction.

Section 8 requires the Recipient, upon Oceaneering’s written request, to promptly either:

1. deliver to Oceaneering and cease to use all Confidential Information in its possession, custody, or control, including that of its Representatives; or  
2. destroy the Confidential Information and delete all electronic records containing it.

The Agreement does not include an express exception allowing the Recipient to retain some Confidential Information after return or destruction.
