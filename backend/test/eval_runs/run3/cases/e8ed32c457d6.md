## Query

Consider QuickBooks's Non-Disclosure Agreement; Does the document specify whether the Receiving Party is required to destroy or return Confidential Information upon the termination of the Agreement?

## Gold

Any materials or documents that have been furnished by one party to the other will be promptly returned, accompanied by all copies of such documentation, after the business possibility has been rejected or concluded.

## RAG Answer

Yes, but only partially. The document specifies that any materials or documents furnished by one party to the other must be promptly returned, along with all copies, after the business possibility has been rejected or concluded. However, it does not expressly require destruction of Confidential Information, nor does it explicitly tie the return obligation to the termination of the Agreement itself.
