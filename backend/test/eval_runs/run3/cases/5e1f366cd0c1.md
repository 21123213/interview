## Query

Consider the Agreement between SE_NDCA and PRE-QUAL PACKAGE; Does the document require that all Confidential Information be expressly identified by the Disclosing Party?

## Gold

All information received from the Company shall be considered Confidential Information, unless it is specifically designated as non-proprietary and non-confidential.

## RAG Answer

No. The Agreement does **not** require the Disclosing Party to expressly identify all Confidential Information.

Under Section 1, **“All information received from the Company shall be considered Confidential Information, unless it is specifically designated as non-proprietary and non-confidential.”** This creates a default rule that information received from the Company is confidential unless the Disclosing Party specifically marks it as non-confidential.
