## Query

Consider Oceaneering's Non-Disclosure Agreement; Does the document allow the Receiving Party to independently develop information that is similar to the Confidential Information?

## Gold

Confidential Information excludes, however, information which:
(iii) is developed by Recipient independently of the information disclosed hereunder.

## RAG Answer

Yes. The agreement excludes from “Confidential Information” any information “developed by Recipient independently of the information disclosed hereunder” (Section 1(iii)). Therefore, the Receiving Party may independently develop information similar to Oceaneering’s Confidential Information, provided it is genuinely independent and not based on or derived from the disclosed Confidential Information.
