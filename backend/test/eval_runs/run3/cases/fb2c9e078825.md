## Query

Consider SINTEC-UK-LTD's Non-Disclosure Agreement; Does the document permit the Receiving Party to retain some Confidential Information even after its return or destruction?

## Gold

The Receiving Party shall return information provided to it any time upon request and, at SINTEC's option, promptly return it to SINTEC once and for all at no charge, or destroy it without retaining any copies or records thereof.
At SINTEC's request, the Receiving Party shall return or destroy any reproductions as stipulated in 5.1.
The Receiving Party shall return to SINTEC any and all information disclosed by SINTEC as well as any copies thereof automatically upon termination/expiration of the business relation- ship.

## RAG Answer

No. Based on the provided NDA, the Receiving Party is not permitted to retain Confidential Information after return or destruction.

Clause 5.1 states that upon SINTEC’s request, the Receiving Party must either return the information “once and for all” or destroy it “without retaining any copies or records thereof.” Clause 5.3 further requires the Receiving Party to return “any and all information disclosed by SINTEC as well as any copies thereof” automatically upon termination or expiration of the business relationship.

The document does not contain a provision allowing the Receiving Party to keep some Confidential Information after return or destruction.
