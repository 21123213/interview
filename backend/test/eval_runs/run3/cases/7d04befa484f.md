## RAG Answer

Yes. SupplementOne’s Non-Disclosure Agreement restricts the use of Confidential Information to the purposes stated in the Agreement.

The Agreement also restricts disclosure and reproduction of Confidential Information to what is necessary for that same purpose, and provides that sensitive personal information may not be disclosed or used except as expressly authorized in the Agreement.
