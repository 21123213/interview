## Query

Consider WECC's Non-Disclosure Agreement; Does the document permit the Receiving Party to retain some Confidential Information even after its return or destruction?

## Gold

b. Upon any termination of this Agreement, Data Recipient shall return to WECC all Non-Public Information in Data Recipient’s possession or destroy all Non-Public Information in Data Recipient’s possession and certify to WECC in writing that all Non-Public Information has been returned or destroyed, except as may be otherwise required by law in which case the confidentiality obligations of this Agreement shall survive termination.

## RAG Answer

Yes, but only in a limited circumstance.

Under Section 7(b) of the WECC Confidentiality Agreement, upon termination the Data Recipient must return or destroy all Non-Public Information and certify that it has done so. However, the document provides an exception: if return or destruction is not permitted because retention is otherwise required by law, the Data Recipient may retain the information, and the confidentiality obligations survive termination.
