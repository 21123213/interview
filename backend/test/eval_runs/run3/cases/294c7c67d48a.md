## Query

Consider Petrolube's Non-Disclosure Agreement; Does the document allow the Receiving Party to share some Confidential Information with their employees?

## Gold

3.2) In consideration of the provision to it of, and its right to use, Samples, Petro-Lube agrees:
3.2.4) to use all reasonable means to keep the Samples secure and limit dissemination of Samples to those of its employees who require access to the Samples for the purpose of Evaluation and, in any event, subject to Clause 3.3.

## RAG Answer

Yes — but only in a limited way.

Under the Petrolube NDA, Petro-Lube, as the Receiving Party, may disseminate Samples to its employees who require access to them for the purpose of Evaluation, subject to Clause 3.2.4. Clause 3.3 further requires Petro-Lube to ensure that any employee receiving Samples is made aware of their confidential nature and agrees to be bound by secrecy/restricted-use obligations no less stringent than those in the Agreement.

So, the document allows sharing of Samples with employees on a need-to-know basis for Evaluation, but it does not expressly permit broader disclosure of other confidential information to employees beyond that condition.
