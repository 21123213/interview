## Query

Consider the Confidentiality Agreement between QEP and BMO; Does the document allow the Receiving Party to share some Confidential Information with their employees?

## Gold

The Confidential Information may be disclosed by the Receiving Company to any of the Receiving Company’s Representatives, but only if and to the limited extent that such Representative needs to know the Confidential Information for the purpose of evaluating the potential Transaction.
(b) For purposes of this Agreement, (i) a person’s “Representatives” shall be the Affiliates, and the officers, directors, managers, members, shareholders, partners, employees, agents, representatives, consultants, principals, attorneys, accountants, advisors and potential financing sources, of such person, and of each of their Affiliates;

## RAG Answer

Yes. Under Section 1(a), the Receiving Company may disclose Confidential Information to its “Representatives,” and Section 1(b) defines Representatives to include the Receiving Company’s employees.

However, disclosure is allowed only if and to the limited extent that the employee needs to know the Confidential Information for the purpose of evaluating the potential Transaction. Before disclosure, the Receiving Company must inform the employee that the information is confidential and require the employee to agree to be bound by the Agreement. The Receiving Company is also liable for any breach by its Representatives, including employees.
