## Query

Consider the Non-Disclosure Agreement between University of Wisconsin Oshkosh; Does the document allow verbally conveyed information to be considered as Confidential Information?

## Gold

1. All information disclosed by one party to the other to evaluate the Project and/or Purpose that is designated in writing as “Confidential” at the time of disclosure or if disclosed orally is designated in writing as “Confidential” within fifteen (15) days of disclosure is “Confidential Information.”

## RAG Answer

Yes. Under Section 1 of the NDA, information disclosed orally may be considered “Confidential Information” if it is designated in writing as “Confidential” within fifteen (15) days of the oral disclosure.

However, there is some internal ambiguity because Section 6 states that Confidential Information “will be provided in writing and marked ‘Confidential.’” Even so, Section 1 expressly contemplates oral disclosures becoming confidential if later designated in writing within 15 days.
