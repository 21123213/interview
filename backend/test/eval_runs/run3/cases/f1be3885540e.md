## Query

Consider Petrolube's Non-Disclosure Agreement; Does the document indicate that the Agreement does not grant the Receiving Party any rights to the Confidential Information?

## Gold

7.1) Nothing in this Agreement shall be construed as an obligation on either Party to enter into any further agreement of any kind or as granting any license under any patent or other intellectual property rights other than those specifically set out in this Agreement.

## RAG Answer

No — not expressly. The provided excerpts do not contain a clear statement that the Agreement grants the Receiving Party no rights to Confidential Information.

The closest provision is Clause 7.1, which says nothing in the Agreement shall be construed as granting any license under patents or other intellectual property rights except those specifically set out. That limits IP licensing, but it does not expressly deny all rights to Confidential Information.

The Agreement also refers to Petro-Lube’s “right to use” Samples for Evaluation, subject to restrictions, and states that Samples are supplied only for experimental and evaluation purposes. Therefore, the document indicates limited rights and no IP license except as specified, rather than a general statement that no rights to Confidential Information are granted.
