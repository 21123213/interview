## Query

Consider the Tabun Kitchen Investments' Non-Disclosure Agreement; Does the document allow the Receiving Party to independently develop information that is similar to the Confidential Information?

## Gold

Notwithstanding the provisions of this Agreement, the obligations and restrictions set forth herein regarding Confidential Information shall not apply to information that the Receiving Party can establish
(iv) is independently developed by the Receiving Party without the use, reference to or benefit of the Disclosing Party’s Confidential Information.

## RAG Answer

Yes. Under Clause 3(iv) of the Tabun Kitchen Investments NDA, the confidentiality obligations do **not** apply to information that the Receiving Party can establish was **“independently developed … without the use, reference to or benefit of the Disclosing Party’s Confidential Information.”**

So the Receiving Party may independently develop information similar to the Confidential Information, provided it can demonstrate that the development was truly independent and did not use, reference, or benefit from the Confidential Information.
