## Query

Consider Sony Pictures Television's Non-Disclosure Agreement; Does the document allow the Receiving Party to share some Confidential Information with third parties, including consultants, agents, and professional advisors?

## Gold

Each party, as a Receiving Party, agrees that it will:
(c) not disclose such Confidential Information to any person other than to those of its and/or its affiliated companies’ respective employees, stockholders, partners, members, directors, officers, advisors, agents, accountants, attorneys, representatives and/or consultants (collectively, “Representatives”), in each case, who (i) need to know such Confidential Information to facilitate the evaluation, negotiation and, if applicable, consummation of the Potential Transaction, and (ii) are advised of the confidential and proprietary nature of such Confidential Information and are bound by confidentiality obligations (which may be contained in such Representatives’ engagement agreements) that limit the further use and disclosure of such Confidential Information;

## RAG Answer

Yes. Under Section 3(c) of the Sony Pictures Television NDA, the Receiving Party may disclose Confidential Information to certain “Representatives,” including its affiliated companies’ employees, stockholders, partners, members, directors, officers, advisors, agents, accountants, attorneys, representatives, and/or consultants.

However, this sharing is limited. The Representatives may receive Confidential Information only if they:

1. need to know it to facilitate the evaluation, negotiation, and, if applicable, consummation of the Potential Transaction; and  
2. are advised of the confidential and proprietary nature of the information and are bound by confidentiality obligations limiting further use and disclosure.

The Receiving Party remains responsible for any breach by its Representatives. The NDA also permits disclosure in certain compelled-by-law situations under Section 4, subject to notice and other conditions.
