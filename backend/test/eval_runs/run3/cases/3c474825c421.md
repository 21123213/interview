## RAG Answer

No. The document does **not** state that Confidential Information shall only include technical information.

Under Section 1, “Confidential Information” is defined broadly to include **all information**, regardless of form, disclosed in connection with the Transaction. This includes, for example, records, reports, analyses, notes, documentation, knowledge, data, specifications, diagrams, statistics, systems or software, manuals, business plans, operational information or practices, processes, customer lists, concepts, ideas, policies, contractual arrangements, supplier/distributor/customer information, and the existence of the discussions between the parties.
