## Query

Consider QuickBooks's Non-Disclosure Agreement; Does the document permit the Receiving Party to retain some Confidential Information even after its return or destruction?

## Gold

Any materials or documents that have been furnished by one party to the other will be promptly returned, accompanied by all copies of such documentation, after the business possibility has been rejected or concluded.

## RAG Answer

No. The QuickBooks NDA template does not appear to permit the Receiving Party to retain any Confidential Information after return.

Under Section 5, “Return of Materials,” any materials or documents furnished by one party to the other must be promptly returned, “accompanied by all copies of such documentation,” after the business opportunity is rejected or concluded. The document does not include an exception allowing retention of Confidential Information after return or destruction.
