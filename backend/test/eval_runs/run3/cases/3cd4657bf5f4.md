## Query

Consider the Confidentiality Agreement between QEP and BMO; Does the document include a clause that prohibits the Receiving Party from soliciting some of the Disclosing Party's representatives?

## Gold

Until the expiration of one year from the Effective Date, or until the execution of a definitive agreement between the Parties regarding a Transaction that addresses solicitation of employees, whichever is earlier, the Receiving Company shall not, and shall cause its Affiliates not to, (a) directly or indirectly recruit (including as a director or consultant), solicit (including by contracting through an independent contractor, consultant or other third party) or otherwise induce any director, officer, employee or consultant of Owner or its Affiliates to terminate his or her directorship, employment or consultancy, as the case may be, or (b) hire or assist another person or entity in hiring or engaging as a consultant any officer, employee or consultant of Owner or its Affiliates.

## RAG Answer

Yes. The document includes a “No Solicitation” clause (Section 11) that prohibits the Receiving Company—and its Affiliates—from directly or indirectly recruiting, soliciting, or inducing any director, officer, employee, or consultant of Owner or Owner’s Affiliates to terminate their role, and from hiring or assisting in hiring such persons, for one year from the Effective Date or until a definitive agreement addressing employee solicitation is executed, whichever is earlier. It contains an exception for hiring persons who respond to public job advertisements.
