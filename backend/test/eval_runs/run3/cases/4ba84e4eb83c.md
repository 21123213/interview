## Query

Consider the Confidentiality Agreement between QEP and BMO; Does the document allow the Receiving Party to acquire information similar to the Confidential Information from a third party?

## Gold

Notwithstanding the foregoing, the following shall not constitute Confidential Information for the purposes of this Agreement:
(c) information which becomes available to the Receiving Company or the Receiving Company’s Representatives on a non-confidential basis from a source other than Owner or any of Owner’s Representatives, provided that such source was not subject to any confidentiality obligation or other prohibition against transmitting the information to the Receiving Company or any of the Receiving Company’s Representatives; and

## RAG Answer

Yes, but only under the conditions set out in the definition of “Confidential Information.”

Under Section 2(a), information that becomes available to the Receiving Company or its Representatives on a **non-confidential basis from a source other than Owner or Owner’s Representatives** is **not** Confidential Information, provided that the source was **not subject to any confidentiality obligation or other prohibition against transmitting the information** to the Receiving Company or its Representatives.

The Agreement also excludes information that is **independently developed by the Receiving Company or its Representatives without use of, or reference to, the Confidential Information**.

So, the document permits the Receiving Party to acquire similar information from a third party, but only if the third-party information is obtained on a non-confidential basis and the third party was not restricted from disclosing it.
