## Query

Consider the Tabun Kitchen Investments' Non-Disclosure Agreement; Does the document state that Confidential Information shall only include technical information?

## Gold

Confidential Information includes, without limitation,
(ii) forecasts, business plans, strategies, and financial statements, records and information, (iii) customer lists or requirements, and (iv) other business or technical information or trade secrets.

## RAG Answer

No. The NDA does not state that Confidential Information is limited to technical information.

Under Clause 1, “Confidential Information” is defined broadly as any and all materials or information not in the public domain that a party receives or acquires from the other party in connection with the agreement, in writing, electronically, orally, visually, or otherwise. It expressly includes, without limitation:

- software, designs, techniques, methods, tools, processes, templates, data, products or services, specifications and plans;
- forecasts, business plans, strategies, financial statements, records and information;
- customer lists or requirements; and
- other business or technical information or trade secrets.

Therefore, Confidential Information covers both business and technical information, not only technical information.
