## Query

Consider SINTEC-UK-LTD's Non-Disclosure Agreement; Does the document allow the Receiving Party to share some Confidential Information with their employees?

## Gold

The Receiving Party will disclose information it receives from SINTEC only to selected members of its staff and only to the extent absolutely necessary for SINTEC's specified or authorized purposes in each case.

## RAG Answer

Yes. Under Section 3 of the SINTEC UK LTD Non-Disclosure Agreement, the Receiving Party may disclose Confidential Information received from SINTEC only to **selected members of its staff**, and only to the extent **absolutely necessary** for SINTEC’s specified or authorized purposes. The Receiving Party must also ensure that those employees are bound by a duty of confidentiality in accordance with the Agreement.
