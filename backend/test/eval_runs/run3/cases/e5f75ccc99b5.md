## Query

Consider RROI's Confidentiality Agreement; Does the document include a clause that prevents the Receiving Party from disclosing the fact that the Agreement was agreed upon or negotiated?

## Gold

Without the prior written consent of the Disclosing Party, the Recipient will not, and will direct its Representatives not to, disclose to any Person other than its Representatives: (i) the fact that any investigations, discussions or negotiations are taking place concerning a possible Transaction; (ii) that the Recipient has requested or received Evaluation Material;
(iv) any of the terms, conditions or any facts with respect to such possible Transaction, including the status thereof.

## RAG Answer

Yes, but only indirectly. Section 1 (“Non-Disclosure”) prohibits the Recipient, without the Disclosing Party’s prior written consent, from disclosing:

- that any investigations, discussions or negotiations are taking place concerning a possible Transaction; and  
- any terms, conditions or facts with respect to such possible Transaction, including its status.

However, the document does not explicitly state that the Receiving Party cannot disclose the fact that the Confidentiality Agreement itself was agreed upon or negotiated.
