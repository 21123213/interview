## Query

Consider SupplementOne's Non-Disclosure Agreement; Does the document require that all Confidential Information be expressly identified by the Disclosing Party?

## Gold

i) “Confidential Information” refers to the following items Discloser discloses to the Recipient:
(3) Any source code and any names of actual or potential Disclosers, whether or not marked as confidential; and
Therefore, notwithstanding (5)(c) above, the Recipient does have an obligation to maintain the confidentiality of such sensitive personal information, whether or not marked as confidential.

## RAG Answer

No. The SupplementOne NDA does not require that **all** Confidential Information be expressly identified by the Disclosing Party.

Although the definition generally covers information that the Discloser designates as “Confidential,” it also expressly includes certain items **whether or not marked as confidential**, such as:

- source code;
- names of actual or potential Disclosers; and
- other nonpublic, sensitive, or third-party information disclosed under the Agreement.

Additionally, certain sensitive personal information remains subject to confidentiality obligations even if it is not marked as confidential.
