## Query

Consider WECC's Non-Disclosure Agreement; Does the document allow the Receiving Party to independently develop information that is similar to the Confidential Information?

## Gold

b. For the purposes of this Agreement, Non-Public Information shall not include:
iii. Information that was or is independently developed by Data Recipient as demonstrated by Data Recipient’s documentation.

## RAG Answer

Yes. The WECC Confidentiality Agreement does not prohibit the receiving party from independently developing information that is similar to Confidential Information.

The document uses the term **“Data Recipient”** rather than “Receiving Party,” and it expressly excludes from the definition of Non-Public Information:

> “Information that was or is independently developed by Data Recipient as demonstrated by Data Recipient’s documentation.”

Therefore, if the Data Recipient can demonstrate through its own documentation that similar information was independently developed, that information is not treated as Non-Public Information under the Agreement. However, the Agreement’s confidentiality obligations still apply to any Non-Public Information actually received from WECC.
