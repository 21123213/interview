## Query

Consider WECC's Non-Disclosure Agreement; Does the document require that all Confidential Information be expressly identified by the Disclosing Party?

## Gold

a. Non-Public Information shall include any and all information Data Recipient receives from WECC, including, but not limited to, information received through a login to the WECC website, which:
i. Is designated as “Confidential Information” or “Market Sensitive Information” or is otherwise considered non-public under the WECC Information Sharing Policy;

## RAG Answer

No. The WECC document does not require that all Confidential Information be expressly identified by the disclosing party.

Under Section 1(a), “Non-Public Information” includes information received from WECC that is:

- designated as “Confidential Information” or “Market Sensitive Information,” or otherwise considered non-public under the WECC Information Sharing Policy;
- meets the definition of Confidential Information in NERC Rules of Procedure Section 1500; or
- identified by WECC as non-public or confidential at the time of disclosure.

Thus, express identification by WECC is only one way information may be covered; it is not a universal requirement for all confidential information.
