## RAG Answer

Yes. The Agreement expressly restricts the use of Confidential Information to the purposes stated in it.

Under Section 2, “Confidential Information shall not be used for any purpose other than to analyze, implement or complete the Transactions.” Disclosure is permitted only to advisors, affiliates, agents, employees, directors, officers, and similar persons with a need-to-know for those same purposes, and those recipients must be bound by the Agreement.

Section 20 further reinforces this restriction by prohibiting the Counterparty from using or disclosing Confidential Information with any third party regarding the Transactions or otherwise to the disadvantage of the Company.
