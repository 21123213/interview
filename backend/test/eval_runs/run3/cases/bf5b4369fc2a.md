## Query

Consider the Tabun Kitchen Investments' Non-Disclosure Agreement; Does the document allow the Receiving Party to share some Confidential Information with their employees?

## Gold

A Receiving Party agrees, acknowledges and undertakes to (i) hold the Confidential Information of the Disclosing Party in strictest confidence, and subject to the terms of this Agreement, not to disclose Confidential Information to any third party unless it is a regulated investment body, solicitor, accountant or private investors known to the Party.

## RAG Answer

No. The provided NDA text does not expressly allow the Receiving Party to share Confidential Information with its employees.

Clause 2 restricts disclosure to third parties, with a limited exception allowing sharing “freely” only with a regulated investment body, solicitor, accountant, or private investors known to the Party. If the intended third party is not known, prior written consent from the Disclosing Party is required. Since employees are not specifically mentioned as permitted recipients, the document does not clearly authorize disclosure to them.
