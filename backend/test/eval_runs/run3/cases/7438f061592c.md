## Query

Consider the Non-Disclosure Agreement between University of Wisconsin Oshkosh; Does the document allow the Receiving Party to share some Confidential Information with third parties, including consultants, agents, and professional advisors?

## Gold

The Receiving Party agrees to disclose Confidential Information only to their respective employees, agents, or representatives who have been determined to have a need to know and have been advised of their obligation to comply with the terms of this Agreement.

## RAG Answer

Yes, but only in a limited way.

Under the NDA, the Receiving Party may disclose Confidential Information **only to its employees, agents, or representatives** who have a **need to know** and have been advised of their obligation to comply with the agreement.

However, the NDA also states that Confidential Information **shall not be provided to any third party without the prior permission of the Disclosing Party**, unless required by law. The document does **not specifically mention consultants or professional advisors**; they would likely need to qualify as “agents” or “representatives,” or the Receiving Party would need the Disclosing Party’s prior permission.
