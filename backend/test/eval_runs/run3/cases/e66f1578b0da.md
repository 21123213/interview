## Query

Consider SupplementOne's Non-Disclosure Agreement; Does the document allow the Receiving Party to acquire information similar to the Confidential Information from a third party?

## Gold

(5) Notwithstanding the foregoing, Confidential Information does not include information that:
(d) Is rightfully received by the Recipient from a third party without an obligation of confidence; or

## RAG Answer

Yes. The NDA allows the Receiving Party to acquire information from a third party if it is **rightfully received without an obligation of confidence**. Under the definition of “Confidential Information,” such third-party information is expressly excluded from the scope of Confidential Information.

However, this does not permit the Receiving Party to use or disclose the Discloser’s Confidential Information itself, or to obtain similar information in violation of the agreement’s confidentiality and use restrictions.
