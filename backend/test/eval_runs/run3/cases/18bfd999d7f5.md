## Query

Consider the Non-Disclosure Agreement between Stony Hill and Buyer; Does the document specify whether the Receiving Party is required to destroy or return Confidential Information upon the termination of the Agreement?

## Gold

(f) If Buyer decides does not purchase the Business, Buyer will promptly return to Broker all Information previously furnished by Broker or Seller, including any and all reproductions of same, and further, shall destroy any and all analyses, compilations or other material that incorporates any part of said Information.

## RAG Answer

Yes, but not expressly “upon termination” of the Agreement.

The NDA states that if the Buyer decides not to purchase the Business, the Buyer must promptly return all Information to the Broker, including all reproductions, and destroy any analyses, compilations, or other materials incorporating any part of the Information.

However, the document does not explicitly say that this return/destruction obligation is triggered by termination of the Agreement itself; it is tied to the Buyer’s decision not to purchase the Business.
