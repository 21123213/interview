## Query

Consider the Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement between TSE, OK, and WHK; Does the document mention that some obligations of the Agreement may survive the termination of the Agreement?

## Gold

The parties shall continue to protect the secrecy of the Confidential Information for as long as the information remains confidential information or a trade secret, but for no less than five (5) years from the date the information to be kept confidential is received or the effective date of this Agreement, whichever is later.

## RAG Answer

Yes. The document does not use the exact phrase “survive termination,” but it indicates that certain obligations continue after the business relationship ends.

Specifically:

- **Section 12 (Term)** states that the parties “shall continue to protect the secrecy of the Confidential Information for as long as the information remains confidential information or a trade secret,” and in any event for **no less than five (5) years** from the later of the date the information was received or the effective date of the Agreement.

- **Section 8 (Return of Confidential Information)** provides that if the business relationship is terminated and the receiving party retains one copy of Confidential Information for permitted purposes, that retained Confidential Information “shall remain subject to the terms and provisions of this Agreement.”

Therefore, the Agreement contemplates that confidentiality and related obligations may continue after termination.
