## Query

Consider Axial's Non-Disclosure Agreement; Does the document specify whether the Receiving Party is required to destroy or return Confidential Information upon the termination of the Agreement?

## Gold

Upon the expiration or termination of this Agreement, or at the Disclosing Party's request at any time during the term of this Agreement, the Recipient and its Representatives shall promptly return to the Disclosing Party all copies, whether in written, electronic or other form or media, of the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information, or destroy all such copies and confirm the same in writing to the Disclosing Party; provided, that the Recipient and its Representatives may retain such Confidential Information as is necessary to enable it to comply with its reasonable document retention policies.

## RAG Answer

Yes. Axial’s standard NDA specifies that upon expiration or termination of the Agreement, the Recipient (i.e., the Receiving Party) and its Representatives must promptly return all copies of the Disclosing Party’s Confidential Information in any form or media, or destroy all such copies and confirm the destruction in writing.

However, the agreement allows the Recipient to retain Confidential Information only as necessary to comply with its reasonable document retention policies.
