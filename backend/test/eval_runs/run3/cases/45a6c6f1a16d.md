## Query

Consider SINTEC-UK-LTD's Non-Disclosure Agreement; Does the document allow the Receiving Party to share some Confidential Information with third parties, including consultants, agents, and professional advisors?

## Gold

The Receiving Party shall treat as strictly confidential any and all information disclosed to it by SINTEC and shall not disclose said information to third parties or use it for its own business purposes or for other customers without the prior written consent of SINTEC.

## RAG Answer

No. The document does **not** generally allow the Receiving Party to share Confidential Information with third parties such as consultants, agents, or professional advisors.

Under the NDA:

- The Receiving Party must keep all disclosed information **strictly confidential** and **shall not disclose it to third parties** without SINTEC’s **prior written consent**.
- Disclosure is expressly limited to **selected members of the Receiving Party’s staff**, and only to the extent absolutely necessary for SINTEC’s specified or authorized purposes.
- The provided clauses do not include any general permission to share Confidential Information with external consultants, agents, or professional advisors.

Therefore, sharing with such third parties would only be permissible if SINTEC gave **prior written consent**; otherwise, it is prohibited.
