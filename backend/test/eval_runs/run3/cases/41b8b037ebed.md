## Query

Consider SINTEC-UK-LTD's Non-Disclosure Agreement; Does the document indicate that the Agreement does not grant the Receiving Party any rights to the Confidential Information?

## Gold

All information disclosed by SINTEC shall remain the property of SINTEC.
Should new, patentable findings be made directly or indirectly in connection with the disclosure of information described in the Preamble, SINTEC shall have the sole and exclusive right to apply for patent protection of such findings and to commercially exploit them.

## RAG Answer

Yes. The Agreement indicates that the Receiving Party is not granted ownership or proprietary rights to the Confidential Information.

In particular:

- Section 5.1 states that “All information disclosed by SINTEC shall remain the property of SINTEC,” and the Receiving Party must return or destroy it upon request or at termination.
- Section 2 restricts the Receiving Party to using the information only for SINTEC’s specified or authorized purposes and prohibits use for its own business purposes or for other customers without SINTEC’s prior written consent.
- Sections 7 and 8 reserve patent and other IP-related rights to SINTEC.

So, while the Receiving Party may have limited rights to use the information for SINTEC’s authorized purposes, the Agreement does not grant it rights of ownership or broader proprietary rights to the Confidential Information.
