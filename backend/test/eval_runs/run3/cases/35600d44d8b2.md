## Query

Consider Simply Fashion's Non-Disclosure Agreement; Does the document allow the Receiving Party to independently develop information that is similar to the Confidential Information?

## Gold

3. This Agreement shall not apply to, and Potential Purchaser shall have no obligation with respect to, any information which
(iv) is independently developed or acquired by Potential Purchaser without violation of this Agreement, or

## RAG Answer

Yes. The NDA’s receiving party is called the “Potential Purchaser,” and Section 3 states that the Agreement does not apply to information that is “independently developed or acquired by Potential Purchaser without violation of this Agreement.”

Therefore, the Potential Purchaser may independently develop information that is similar to the Confidential Information/Evaluation Materials, provided it was developed without using or otherwise violating the Agreement’s confidentiality obligations.
