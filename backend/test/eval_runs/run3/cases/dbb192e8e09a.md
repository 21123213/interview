## Query

Consider Axial's Non-Disclosure Agreement; Does the document allow the Receiving Party to acquire information similar to the Confidential Information from a third party?

## Gold

Except as required by applicable federal, state or local law or regulation, the term "Confidential Information" as used in this Agreement shall not include information that:
(b) at the time of disclosure is, or thereafter becomes, available to the Recipient on a non-confidential basis from a third-party source, provided that such third party is not and was not prohibited from disclosing such Confidential Information to the Recipient by any contractual obligation;

## RAG Answer

Yes. Under Axial’s Standard NDA, “Confidential Information” excludes information that “is, or thereafter becomes, available to the Recipient on a non-confidential basis from a third-party source,” provided that the third party “is not and was not prohibited from disclosing such Confidential Information to the Recipient by any contractual obligation.”

So the Receiving Party may acquire or receive similar information from a third party, but the NDA’s confidentiality restrictions do not apply only if the information is obtained on a non-confidential basis from a third party that is not contractually prohibited from disclosing it.
