## Query

Consider WECC's Non-Disclosure Agreement; Does the document allow the Receiving Party to acquire information similar to the Confidential Information from a third party?

## Gold

b. For the purposes of this Agreement, Non-Public Information shall not include:
ii. Information that was or is acquired by Data Recipient from a source other than WECC in a manner which is not otherwise subject to confidentiality restrictions; or

## RAG Answer

Yes. The agreement excludes from “Non-Public Information” any information that the Data Recipient “acquired from a source other than WECC in a manner which is not otherwise subject to confidentiality restrictions.”

So the Receiving Party may obtain similar information from a third party, provided it is not obtained from WECC and is not subject to confidentiality restrictions.
