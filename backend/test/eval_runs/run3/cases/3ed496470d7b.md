## Query

Consider the Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement between Roundhouse and Creative; Does the document require the Receiving Party to notify the Disclosing Party if they are required by law, regulation, or judicial process to disclose any Confidential Information?

## Gold

The Recipient of Confidential Information disclosed under this Agreement shall promptly notify the disclosing Party of any disclosure of such Confidential Information in violation of this Agreement or of any subpoena or other legal process requiring production or disclosure of said Confidential Information.

## RAG Answer

Yes. Under Clause 2 of the Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement, the Receiving Party (“Recipient”) is required to **promptly notify** the Disclosing Party of **any subpoena or other legal process requiring production or disclosure** of Confidential Information.

However, the document does not expressly use the words “law” or “regulation” in that notification requirement; it specifically refers to a “subpoena or other legal process.”
