## Query

## RAG Answer

No. The document does not state that Confidential Information is limited to technical information only.

Under the definition clause, “Information within the meaning of this Agreement” includes “any and all technical and commercial information,” specifically drawings, plans, specifications, methods, formulae, designs, documentation, calculations, market and customer data, materials, and other objects disclosed by SINTEC directly or indirectly in connection with the business relationship, in oral, visual, written, data-storage or other forms.
