## Query

Consider the Confidentiality Agreement between QEP and BMO; Does the document state that Confidential Information shall only include technical information?

## Gold

(a) For purposes of this Agreement, the term “Confidential Information” means, collectively, any and all information, materials or data (whether written, electronic, video or oral) which concerns, relates to, or is associated in any way with Owner, its Affiliates, the Properties or the Transaction, that is disclosed or made available to the Receiving Company or any of its Representatives by Owner, or any of Owner’s Representatives, which is either confidential, proprietary, or otherwise not generally available to the public, including, without limitation, analyses, interpretations, compilations, reports, reservoir data, geologic and geophysical data, maps, models, financial data, economic data, commercial data, contractual data, environmental data, marketing data, operational data, engineering data, and other information, documents, materials and data, as well as any copies, notes, reports, analyses, compilations, data, studies, excerpts, evaluation materials, summaries or other documents, information, or materials developed or prepared by or on behalf of the Receiving Company or any of its Representatives that include, incorporate, refer to, reflect, are derived from or are based on, in whole or in part, any other Confidential Information.

## RAG Answer

No. The document does **not** limit Confidential Information to technical information.

Under **Section 2(a)** of the Confidentiality Agreement, “Confidential Information” is defined broadly to include **any and all information, materials or data**—written, electronic, video, or oral—that concerns, relates to, or is associated with Owner, its Affiliates, the Properties, or the Transaction, provided it is confidential, proprietary, or otherwise not generally available to the public.

The definition expressly includes, without limitation:

- analyses, interpretations, compilations, reports;
- reservoir data;
- geologic and geophysical data;
- maps and models;
- financial data;
- economic data;
- commercial data;
- contractual data;
- environmental data;
- marketing data;
- operational data;
- engineering data;
- and other information, documents, materials, and data;

as well as copies, notes, reports, analyses, compilations, data, studies, excerpts, evaluation materials, summaries, or other materials prepared by or for the Receiving Company that include, refer to, reflect, are derived from, or are based on Confidential Information.

Therefore, Confidential Information under the agreement includes more than technical information; it also includes financial, commercial, contractual, environmental, marketing, operational, engineering, and other categories of information.
