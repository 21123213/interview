## Query

Consider SINTEC-UK-LTD's Non-Disclosure Agreement; Does the document specify whether the Receiving Party is required to destroy or return Confidential Information upon the termination of the Agreement?

## Gold

The Receiving Party shall return to SINTEC any and all information disclosed by SINTEC as well as any copies thereof automatically upon termination/expiration of the business relation- ship.

## RAG Answer

Yes.

The document specifies that:

- Under **Clause 5.1**, all information disclosed by SINTEC remains SINTEC’s property, and the Receiving Party must return it upon request. At SINTEC’s option, the Receiving Party must either return it to SINTEC or destroy it without retaining any copies or records.

- Under **Clause 5.2**, any reproductions of SINTEC’s information must also be returned or destroyed at SINTEC’s request, as stipulated in Clause 5.1.

- Under **Clause 5.3**, the Receiving Party must return **all** information disclosed by SINTEC, and any copies of it, **automatically upon termination or expiration of the business relationship**.
