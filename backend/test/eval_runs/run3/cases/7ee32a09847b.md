## Query

Consider the Confidentiality Agreement between QEP and BMO; Does the document allow the Receiving Party to share some Confidential Information with third parties, including consultants, agents, and professional advisors?

## Gold

The Confidential Information may be disclosed by the Receiving Company to any of the Receiving Company’s Representatives, but only if and to the limited extent that such Representative needs to know the Confidential Information for the purpose of evaluating the potential Transaction.
(b) For purposes of this Agreement, (i) a person’s “Representatives” shall be the Affiliates, and the officers, directors, managers, members, shareholders, partners, employees, agents, representatives, consultants, principals, attorneys, accountants, advisors and potential financing sources, of such person, and of each of their Affiliates;

## RAG Answer

Yes. Under Section 1(a), the Receiving Company may disclose Confidential Information to its “Representatives,” but only to the extent such Representative needs to know it for the purpose of evaluating the potential Transaction.

Section 1(b) defines “Representatives” to include agents, consultants, attorneys, accountants, advisors, and similar persons. Before disclosure, the Receiving Company must inform them of the confidential nature of the information, require them to agree to be bound by the Agreement, and remains liable for their breach.
