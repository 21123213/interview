## Query

Consider the Confidentiality Agreement between Tazza and CAFFE; Does the document restrict the use of Confidential Information to the purposes stated in the Agreement?

## Gold

The potential Franchisee hereby agrees to respect unconditionally the confidential nature of all confidential information and to never disclose or communicate any confidential information to a third party, be it directly or indirectly, or knowingly use this information for any purpose whatsoever, except with the Franchisor’s written consent.

## RAG Answer

Yes. The Confidentiality Agreement restricts the use of Confidential Information.

Under Section 2 (“Non-disclosure”), the potential Franchisee agrees not to “knowingly use this information for any purpose whatsoever,” except with the Franchisor’s written consent. The Agreement’s background also indicates that the information is provided to allow the potential Franchisee to evaluate a possible Franchise Agreement or transaction, but the operative restriction is broader: any use of Confidential Information is prohibited unless the Franchisor gives written consent.
