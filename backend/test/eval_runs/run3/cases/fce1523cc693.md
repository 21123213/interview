## Query

Consider Axial's Non-Disclosure Agreement; Does the document allow the Receiving Party to independently develop information that is similar to the Confidential Information?

## Gold

Except as required by applicable federal, state or local law or regulation, the term "Confidential Information" as used in this Agreement shall not include information that:
(d) was or is independently developed by the Recipient, as established by documentary evidence, without reference to Confidential Information; or

## RAG Answer

Yes. Under Axial’s Standard NDA, information that is **independently developed by the Recipient** is excluded from “Confidential Information” if it is:

- **established by documentary evidence**, and  
- developed **without reference to the Confidential Information**.

So the Recipient may independently develop information that is similar to the Confidential Information, provided the development is truly independent and not based on, derived from, or informed by the Confidential Information.

However, the NDA also prohibits using Confidential Information for purposes other than the agreed Purpose, including to **reverse engineer, disassemble, decompile, or design around** confidential intellectual property. It also does not grant the Recipient any license to the Disclosing Party’s intellectual property rights.
