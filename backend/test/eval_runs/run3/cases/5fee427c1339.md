## RAG Answer

Yes. The Agreement restricts use of Confidential Information to the stated Purpose.

Specifically, it states that the Receiving Party “shall use the Confidential Information solely in connection with the Purpose,” and access is limited to employees, agents, representatives, consultants, contractors, and certain third-party vendors who reasonably require access for that Purpose.

The Purpose is defined as evaluating whether to enter into a business relationship and related projects, and, if such a relationship is commenced, exchanging Confidential Information in connection with that relationship. Limited exceptions exist, such as disclosure required by law, subpoena, or court order, and use of third-party vendors for obtaining price quotations.
