## Query

Consider the Non-Disclosure Agreement between NREL and Another Company; Does the document restrict the use of Confidential Information to the purposes stated in the Agreement?

## Gold

b. Receiving Party will treat Proprietary Information and NREL Protected Information that is within the Scope and that is disclosed in compliance with Paragraph 2.a, above, as confidential and proprietary and will use such information only for the Purpose.

## RAG Answer

Yes. The NREL bilateral Non-Disclosure Agreement restricts use of the confidential information to the stated “Purpose.”

Specifically:

- Under Section 1(d), the Parties furnish information for the purpose described in the Agreement, defined as the “Purpose.”
- Under Section 2(b), the Receiving Party “will use such information only for the Purpose.”
- Under Section 2(c), access is limited to employees, agents, and independent contractors who need access “specifically related to the Purpose.”
- Under Section 4(a), no license or right is granted except to use the information in the manner and extent authorized by the Agreement.

So, confidential information may only be used for the purposes stated in the Agreement.
