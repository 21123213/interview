## Gold

As used herein, “Proprietary Information” means information that (i) embodies trade secrets as defined under 18 U.S.C. § 1839 or (ii) is commercial or financial information that is privileged or confidential under the Freedom of Information Act (5 U.S.C. § 552(b)(4)), and that is developed at private expense outside this Agreement.

No. The document does not state that Confidential Information is limited to technical information.
