## RAG Answer

Yes. SupplementOne’s Non-Disclosure Agreement expressly states that certain obligations survive termination.

Specifically, the document says:

- “The provisions of this Section 1)b) shall survive the termination of this Agreement.”  
  This refers to the restrictions on use and disclosure of Confidential Information.

- “The provisions of this Section 1)d) shall survive the termination of this Agreement.”  
  This refers to the obligation to return or certify destruction of Confidential Information upon Discloser’s request or termination.

So the Agreement does mention that some obligations may survive termination.
