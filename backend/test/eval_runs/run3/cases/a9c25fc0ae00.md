## RAG Answer

No, the document does **not** generally allow the Receiving Party to share Confidential Information with third parties such as consultants, agents, or professional advisors.

Under the NDA’s restrictions:

- The Receiving Party may disclose Confidential Information only to an **employee or subcontractor** who:
  1. needs access to facilitate evaluation and preparation of a response to RFP CSP903918; and  
  2. executes a nondisclosure agreement with terms no less restrictive than the NDA.

- The Receiving Party **shall not disclose Confidential Information to any other third party** without the Discloser’s **prior written consent**.

There are limited exceptions, including disclosure required by applicable law or legal/governmental authority, with prompt notice to the Discloser, and disclosure with the Discloser’s written consent. But the provided document does not contain a general permission to share Confidential Information with consultants, agents, or professional advisors.
