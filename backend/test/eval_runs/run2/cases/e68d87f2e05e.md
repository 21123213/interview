## Query

Consider Kerber's Non-Disclosure Agreement; Does the document allow the Receiving Party to acquire information similar to the Confidential Information from a third party?

## Gold

4. The restrictions above will not apply to Confidential Information which:
(c) The receiving party obtains from a third party under conditions permitting its disclosure to others;

## RAG Answer

Yes. Under Section 4(c) of Kerber’s Non-Disclosure Agreement, the confidentiality restrictions do not apply to information that the receiving party obtains from a third party under conditions permitting its disclosure to others.

So, if the receiving party acquires information similar to Confidential Information from a third party in a manner that allows further disclosure, the Agreement permits that acquisition and use, subject to the third party’s conditions.
