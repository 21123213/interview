## Query

Consider the Non-Disclosure Agreement between IBC and PMS; Does the document require the Receiving Party to notify the Disclosing Party if they are required by law, regulation, or judicial process to disclose any Confidential Information?

## Gold

(b) Vendor may disclose Confidential Information in accordance with a Canadian judicial or other legal binding Canadian governmental order, provided that Vendor either (i) gives IBC reasonable notice prior to such disclosure to allow IBC a reasonable opportunity to seek a protective order or equivalent, or

## RAG Answer

Yes, but not as an unconditional requirement.

Under Section 2(b) of the IBC–PMS NDA, if the Receiving Party (Vendor) is required to disclose Confidential Information pursuant to a **Canadian judicial or other legally binding Canadian governmental order**, it may disclose it only if it **either**:

1. **gives IBC reasonable notice prior to the disclosure**, to allow IBC a reasonable opportunity to seek a protective order or equivalent; **or**
2. **obtains written assurance** from the applicable judicial or governmental entity that the Confidential Information will be afforded the highest level of protection available under applicable law or regulation.

So, notification is required for compelled disclosure **unless** Vendor instead obtains the specified written assurance from the authority.
