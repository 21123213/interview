## Query

Consider the Non-Disclosure Agreement between GWI and Technohubs; Does the document require that all Confidential Information be expressly identified by the Disclosing Party?

## Gold

2.1 Information, documents, software, objects and other materials to be kept secret (hereinafter: "confidential information") shall be designated as such in writing by the disclosing party. Written designation shall be in the form of an unmistakable annotation such as secret, confidential, for restricted internal access only, etc. Orally given information need only be kept secret if designated as confidential information at the time of its disclosure and supplied in addition within 14 (fourteen) days to the receiving party in summarised written form marked as confidential.

## RAG Answer

Yes. Under Section 2.1 of the NDA, Confidential Information must be expressly designated as confidential by the Disclosing Party in writing, using an unmistakable annotation such as “secret,” “confidential,” or “for restricted internal access only.”

For orally disclosed information, the NDA requires that it be designated as confidential at the time of disclosure and then confirmed in summarized written form, marked as confidential, within 14 days.
