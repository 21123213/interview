## Query

Consider the Non-Disclosure Agreement between Sqiller and Beta Testers; Does the document allow the Receiving Party to acquire information similar to the Confidential Information from a third party?

## Gold

1.2. “Confidential Information” means information related to a Party, its Group Companies, its Authorized Persons, this Agreement or any agreement to which this Agreement is annexed or upon which this Agreement’s Purpose is based, that a Party receives or accesses, except information that
(iv) is obtained by the Receiving Party from third parties without any obligation of confidentiality to the Disclosing Party.

## RAG Answer

Yes. Under clause 1.2 of the NDA, “Confidential Information” excludes information that is obtained by the Receiving Party from third parties without any obligation of confidentiality to the Disclosing Party. Therefore, the Receiving Party may acquire similar information from a third party if it is obtained without a confidentiality obligation to the Disclosing Party.
