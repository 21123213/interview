## Query

Consider Fintricity's Non-Disclosure Agreement; Does the document permit the Receiving Party to retain some Confidential Information even after its return or destruction?

## Gold

Notwithstanding the foregoing each recipient may retain one copy of all Materials containing Confidential Information of the disclosing party received or made in connection with this Agreement for archival purposes only, subject always to strict compliance with the obligations of Clauses 3 and 5.

## RAG Answer

Yes. Under Clause 9 of the Fintricity NDA, although the Receiving Party must generally return or destroy Confidential Information after completion or termination of the Business Purpose, it may retain **one copy of all Materials containing Confidential Information** for **archival purposes only**, subject to strict compliance with the confidentiality obligations in Clauses 3 and 5.
