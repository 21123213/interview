## Query

Consider Fintricity's Non-Disclosure Agreement; Does the document restrict the use of Confidential Information to the purposes stated in the Agreement?

## Gold

Each party may disclose Confidential Information received from the other party to other members of the recipient's company for use only in connection with the Business Purpose and each party shall be responsible for observance of the provisions of this Agreement by such other members of its respective group.

## RAG Answer

Yes. Fintricity’s NDA restricts the use of Confidential Information to the stated purpose of the Agreement.

Specifically:

- **Clause 1(e)** defines “Confidential Information” as information disclosed **in connection with the Business Purpose specified in the Schedule**.
- **Clause 3(d)** states that a recipient must **not use or circulate Confidential Information within its own organisation except solely to the extent necessary for the purposes of the Business Purpose**, or for any other purpose that the disclosing party expressly authorises in writing.
- **Clause 6** further provides that Confidential Information may be disclosed to other members of the recipient’s company **only for use in connection with the Business Purpose**.

So, the Agreement limits use of Confidential Information to the Business Purpose set out in the Agreement, unless the disclosing party gives prior written authorisation for another purpose.
