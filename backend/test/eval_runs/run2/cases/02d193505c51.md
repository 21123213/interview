## Query

Consider New York City Bar Association's Non-Disclosure Agreement; Does the document allow verbally conveyed information to be considered as Confidential Information?

## Gold

(b) “Evaluation Material” means any information or data concerning the Company or any of its Affiliates, whether in oral, visual, written, electronic or other form, that is disclosed to Buyer or any of its Representatives [before the date hereof,]6 now or in the future by or on behalf of the Company or any of its Representatives, together with all notes, memoranda, forecasts, summaries, analyses, compilations and other writings relating thereto that are prepared by or on behalf of Buyer or any of its Representatives to the extent that they use, contain, reflect or are derived from or incorporate, in whole or in part, any such information or data.

## RAG Answer

Yes.

Although the model NDA uses the term **“Evaluation Material”** rather than “Confidential Information,” it expressly covers information conveyed orally.

Under Section 1(b), **“Evaluation Material”** means:

> “any information or data concerning the Company or any of its Affiliates, **whether in oral, visual, written, electronic or other form**, that is disclosed to Buyer or any of its Representatives … by or on behalf of the Company or any of its Representatives…”

Section 2(a) then requires Buyer and its Representatives to keep all Evaluation Material strictly confidential, except as permitted by the agreement.

So, **verbally conveyed information can be treated as confidential information under the NDA if it qualifies as Evaluation Material**, subject to the stated exclusions, such as information independently developed, publicly available, or already lawfully in Buyer’s possession.
