## Query

Consider Inaturals's Non-Disclosure Agreement; Does the document allow the Receiving Party to share some Confidential Information with third parties, including consultants, agents, and professional advisors?

## Gold

The Recipient will communicate such information only to such employees and contractors or agents who have a need to know such information, and only to such employees and contractors or agents having written agreements with the Recipient obligating them to keep such information confidential.

## RAG Answer

Yes, but only in a limited way.

Under the NDA, the Receiving Party may communicate Confidential Information **only to employees and contractors or agents** who:

1. have a **need to know** the information, and  
2. have **written confidentiality agreements** with the Receiving Party.

The document does **not expressly allow** sharing with **consultants or professional advisors** unless they qualify as “contractors or agents” and meet those conditions. Otherwise, the NDA generally prohibits disclosure to third parties without the other party’s prior written consent, subject to limited exceptions such as information already in the public domain or required by law.
