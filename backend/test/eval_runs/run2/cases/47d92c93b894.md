## Query

Consider the Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement between IPTK and CO; Does the document allow the Receiving Party to share some Confidential Information with third parties, including consultants, agents, and professional advisors?

## Gold

Except as described elsewhere in this Agreement, the Recipient and those of its employees, officers, agents and affiliates permitted access hereunder will hold the Confidential Information in confidence and will take all necessary steps to pre-serve the confidential and proprietary nature of the Confidential Information. Without limiting the foregoing, the Recipient (1) will not disclose any Confidential Information to persons within its organization who do not have a need to know in order for the Recipient to engage in the Purpose; (2) will not disclose any Confidential Information to any person outside of its organization unless such person has a need to know in order for the Recipient to engage in the Purpose, and such person is bound by fiduci-ary or contractual duties of confidentiality to the Recipient that are at least as stringent as those contained in this Agreement;

## RAG Answer

Yes. Under Section 4 (“Nondisclosure”) of the IPTK–CO Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement, the Receiving Party may share Confidential Information with third parties, including agents and attorneys/professional advisors, but only if:

1. the third party has a **need to know** the information for the Agreement’s stated Purpose; and  
2. the third party is bound by fiduciary or contractual confidentiality duties that are **at least as stringent** as those in the Agreement.

The Agreement also requires the Receiving Party, if reasonably required, to cause employees, officers, agents, affiliates, and attorneys with access to sign a confidentiality/non-disclosure agreement for the benefit of the Discloser. Consultants are not expressly listed in the permitted-access language, but they may qualify as outside third parties or agents if the above conditions are satisfied.
