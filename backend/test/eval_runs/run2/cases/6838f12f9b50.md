## Query

Consider Kenway's Non-Disclosure Agreement; Does the document allow verbally conveyed information to be considered as Confidential Information?

## Gold

“Confidential Information:” means all confidential information (however recorded, preserved or disclosed) disclosed by a Party or its Representatives to the other Party and that Party's Representatives including but not limited to: the fact that discussions and negotiations are taking place concerning the Purpose and the status of those discussions and negotiations; any information that would be regarded as confidential by a reasonable business person relating to:

## RAG Answer

Yes. Under the Kenway NDA, “Confidential Information” is defined broadly as “all confidential information (however recorded, preserved or disclosed)” disclosed by a Party or its Representatives. That wording is broad enough to cover information conveyed verbally, provided it otherwise meets the definition and is not within one of the listed exclusions.
