## Query

Consider NCDG's Non-Disclosure Agreement; Does the document require that all Confidential Information be expressly identified by the Disclosing Party?

## Gold

4. Such Confidential Information shall also include but shall not be limited to:
a) information disclosed by the Disclosing Party in writing marked as confidential at the time of disclosure;
b) information disclosed by the Disclosing Party orally which is slated to be confidential at the time of disclosure;
c) information disclosed in any other manner is designated in writing as Confidential Information at the time of disclosure; or
d) notwithstanding sub-clauses a,b & c of this definition, any information whose nature makes it obvious that it is confidential.

## RAG Answer

No. The NCDG Non-Disclosure Agreement does not require that **all** Confidential Information be expressly identified or marked by the Disclosing Party.

Under the definition of “Confidential Information,” the Agreement includes information disclosed in writing marked as confidential, oral information “slated” as confidential, and other information designated in writing as confidential at the time of disclosure. However, it also expressly states that Confidential Information includes, **notwithstanding those marking/designation requirements**, “any information whose nature makes it obvious that it is confidential.”

Therefore, express identification is required for certain categories of disclosed information, but not for all Confidential Information; information that is obviously confidential by its nature is covered even without explicit designation.
