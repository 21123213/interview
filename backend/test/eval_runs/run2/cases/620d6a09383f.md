## Query

Consider Kerber's Non-Disclosure Agreement; Does the document require that all Confidential Information be expressly identified by the Disclosing Party?

## Gold

1. For the purpose of this Agreement, “Confidential Information” means information, technical or commercial, whether in visual or machine readable form, received by one party from the other which is marked “Confidential” or “Proprietary”, or which would logically be considered confidential or proprietary in view of its relationship to the whole disclosure.

## RAG Answer

No. The agreement does not require that **all** Confidential Information be expressly identified by the Disclosing Party.

Under Section 1, Confidential Information includes information that is:

- **marked “Confidential” or “Proprietary,”** or  
- **would logically be considered confidential or proprietary** in view of its relationship to the whole disclosure.

However, for information initially furnished orally, the disclosing party must identify it as confidential or proprietary at the time of disclosure and confirm it in writing within 30 days.
