## Query

Consider JB Machine LLC's Non-Disclosure Agreement; Does the document require that all Confidential Information be expressly identified by the Disclosing Party?

## Gold

The nature of the information and manner of the disclosure are such that a reasonable person would understand to be confidential.

## RAG Answer

No. The agreement does not require that all Confidential Information be expressly identified or marked by the Disclosing Party.

The “Confidential Information” section defines it broadly, including information provided directly or indirectly by the Owner, information that a reasonable person would understand to be confidential, and even information “not to be clearly classified as such” if connected to the contract. The agreement also requires protection of information that may not fit the definition if disclosure could harm the Owner.
