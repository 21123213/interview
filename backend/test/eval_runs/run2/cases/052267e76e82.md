## Query

Consider the Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement between IPTK and CO; Does the document permit the Receiving Party to retain some Confidential Information even after its return or destruction?

## Gold

Upon the written request of the Discloser, the Recipient shall either destroy or return to the Discloser any and all Confidential Information in the Recipient’s possession, except for one copy, which copy may be retained for archival purposes in a secure file.

## RAG Answer

是的。根据第10条“Return of Information”，在披露方书面要求下，接收方应返还或销毁其持有的全部保密信息，但可保留一份用于存档目的的副本，并须保存在安全文件中；错误转发的副本则必须全部销毁，不得保留存档副本。
