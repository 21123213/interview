## Query

Consider the Non-Disclosure Agreement between Dollar General and Portfolio Entity; Does the document allow the Receiving Party to share some Confidential Information with their employees?

## Gold

Buyer shall keep the Confidential Materials confidential and shall not disclose any of the Confidential Materials in any manner whatsoever, provided, however, that
(ii) any information contained in the Confidential Materials may be disclosed to Buyer’s Representatives who need to know that information for the purpose of evaluating a possible Transaction with Owner and who agree to keep that information confidential.

## RAG Answer

The provided document does **not expressly say “employees.”**

It permits the Buyer (the Receiving Party) to disclose Confidential Materials to **Buyer’s Representatives** who:

1. **need to know** the information for evaluating a possible transaction, and  
2. **agree to keep the information confidential**.

So, sharing with employees is allowed **only if those employees qualify as “Representatives”** under the agreement. The provided excerpts do not define “Representatives,” so it cannot be confirmed directly that employees are permitted.
