## Query

Consider INFOMAGNET's Non-Disclosure Agreement; Does the document permit the Receiving Party to create a copy of some Confidential Information under certain circumstances?

## Gold

(c) Confidential Information and Confidential Materials may be disclosed, reproduced, summarised, or distributed only in pursuance of the Receiving Party's business relationship with Disclosing Party, and only as otherwise provided hereunder.

## RAG Answer

Yes. Under the INFOMAGNET Two-Way Non-Disclosure Agreement, the Receiving Party may create a copy of Confidential Information only in limited circumstances.

Clause 2(c) states that Confidential Information and Confidential Materials may be “disclosed, reproduced, summarised, or distributed only in pursuance of the Receiving Party’s business relationship with Disclosing Party, and only as otherwise provided hereunder.”

So copying/reproduction is permitted only when it is connected to the Receiving Party’s business relationship with the Disclosing Party and is otherwise allowed by the Agreement. The Receiving Party must also:

- keep the information confidential using reasonable security precautions;
- disclose it only to employees or bona-fide consultants on a need-to-know basis;
- ensure those employees/consultants are bound by appropriate written agreements;
- segregate Confidential Materials to prevent accidental disclosure; and
- return or certify destruction of originals, copies, reproductions, and summaries when required under Clause 3(b).
