## Query

Consider Inaturals's Non-Disclosure Agreement; Does the document permit the Receiving Party to retain some Confidential Information even after its return or destruction?

## Gold

7. Upon written request from the other party, each party agrees to return or destroy all the other party’s Confidential Information, together with all copies or reproductions thereof.

## RAG Answer

No. Based on the provided NDA, the Receiving Party is not expressly permitted to retain Confidential Information after return or destruction.

Clause 4 requires that, upon request, each party promptly return all documents or other tangible property containing Confidential Information or destroy any Confidential Information in tangible form in its possession. Clause 7 further requires each party, upon written request, to return or destroy all of the other party’s Confidential Information, together with all copies or reproductions thereof.

The agreement does exclude certain information from the confidentiality/non-use obligations, such as information already in the receiving party’s possession, public information, information received from a third party, or information required by law to be disclosed. However, those exclusions do not create an express right to retain Confidential Information after return or destruction.
