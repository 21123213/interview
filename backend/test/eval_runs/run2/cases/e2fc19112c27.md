## Query

Consider NCDG's Non-Disclosure Agreement; Does the document restrict the use of Confidential Information to the purposes stated in the Agreement?

## Gold

The Receiving Party is entitled to use the Confidential Information but only for the Purpose.

## RAG Answer

Yes.

Under NCDG’s Non-Disclosure Agreement, the use of Confidential Information is expressly restricted to the purposes stated in the Agreement. Specifically, Section 9 (“Use of Confidential Information”) provides that the Receiving Party “is entitled to use the Confidential Information but only for the Purpose.”

The “Purpose” is defined in the Agreement as the evaluations, discussions, negotiations, and execution regarding a contractual relationship between the Parties in respect of the Project.
