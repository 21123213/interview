## Query

Consider the Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement between Khronos and Khronos; Does the document require that all Confidential Information be expressly identified by the Disclosing Party?

## Gold

”Confidential Information” shall include all technical and non-technical information provided by either party to the other, including but not limited to materials generated by Khronos and by Members on behalf of Khronos, and not specifically designated as non-Confidential by the providing party, including all versions and revisions of draft specifications and any passwords and minutes provided to either Participant or Khronos under this agreement.

## RAG Answer

No. The Khronos Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement does **not** require that all Confidential Information be expressly identified or designated as confidential by the Disclosing Party.

Under Section 2, “Confidential Information” is defined broadly to include technical and non-technical information provided by either party, **unless it is specifically designated as non-Confidential by the providing party**. Thus, information is generally treated as Confidential by default, subject to the listed exclusions such as public domain information, independently developed information, or information rightfully received from a third party without confidentiality obligations.
