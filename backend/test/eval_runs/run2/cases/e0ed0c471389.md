## Query

Consider New York City Bar Association's Non-Disclosure Agreement; Does the document mention that some obligations of the Agreement may survive the termination of the Agreement?

## Gold

All such Evaluation Material retained in accordance with the foregoing proviso shall continue to be subject to Section 2 [for so long as any Evaluation Material is so retained].
12. Term. Except for Sections 13(i) (Governing Law; Forum), 13(j) (WAIVER OF JURY TRIAL) and 13(k) (Conflict Waiver), which shall be binding in perpetuity or until the latest date permitted by Applicable Law, [the last sentence of Section 5]28 and Sections 7 and 8 which shall survive in accordance with their respective terms, this Agreement shall expire upon the date that is [___] year[s] after the date of this Agreement.

## RAG Answer

Yes. The document includes a survival provision in **Section 12 (“Term”)**. It states that, except for certain provisions, the Agreement expires after a specified period, but some obligations continue after expiration/termination.

Specifically, it says that:

- **Section 13(i)** — Governing Law; Forum  
- **Section 13(j)** — Waiver of Jury Trial  
- **Section 13(k)** — Conflict Waiver  

shall be **binding in perpetuity or until the latest date permitted by Applicable Law**.

It also states that:

- the **last sentence of Section 5** — regarding retained Evaluation Material remaining subject to Section 2 for so long as it is retained, and  
- **Sections 7 and 8** — regarding no solicitation/hiring and standstill obligations,  

shall **survive in accordance with their respective terms**.
