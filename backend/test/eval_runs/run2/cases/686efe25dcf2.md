## Query

Consider the Non-Disclosure Agreement between GWI and Technohubs; Does the document allow the Receiving Party to share some Confidential Information with third parties, including consultants, agents, and professional advisors?

## Gold

All confidential information made available by one party to the other shall be kept secret from third parties.
No confidential information received may be supplied to a third party without the prior written permission of the other party hereto.
Disclosure of confidential information to affiliated companies is permitted under the conditions laid out in 5.1 above.

## RAG Answer

No. Based on the provided NDA, the Receiving Party is generally **not allowed** to share Confidential Information with third parties, including consultants, agents, or professional advisors.

Clause 3.2 states that **no confidential information may be supplied to a third party without the prior written permission** of the other party. The only permitted disclosures are to **employees** and **affiliated companies** under Clause 5, where necessary for the purpose of the agreement. There is no clause allowing disclosure to consultants, agents, or professional advisors.
