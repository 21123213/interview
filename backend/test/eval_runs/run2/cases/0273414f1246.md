## Query

Consider the Non-Disclosure Agreement between Gar and Kenyon; Does the document allow verbally conveyed information to be considered as Confidential Information?

## Gold

Information disclosed in other that written form shall be considered Confidential Information only to the extent GK summarizes the same in a written form that is transmitted to SUPPLIER within thirty (30) calendar days of the non-written disclosures.

## RAG Answer

Yes, but only conditionally.

Under the NDA, information disclosed in a non-written form (such as verbally) is considered Confidential Information only if Gar Kenyon summarizes that information in writing and transmits the written summary to the Supplier within 30 calendar days of the non-written disclosure. Otherwise, verbally conveyed information is not automatically treated as Confidential Information.
