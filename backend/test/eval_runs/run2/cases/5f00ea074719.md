## Query

Consider INFOMAGNET's Non-Disclosure Agreement; Does the document mention whether or not the Receiving Party is allowed to reverse engineer any objects which embody the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information?

## Gold

## RAG Answer

Yes. The NDA addresses reverse engineering in Section 2(d):

> “Receiving Party may not reverse engineer, decompile or disassemble any software disclosed to Receiving Party.”

So the Receiving Party is **not allowed to reverse engineer any software disclosed** under the agreement. However, the document does not expressly extend this prohibition to all “objects” embodying Confidential Information generally.
