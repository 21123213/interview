## Query

Consider ROI Corporation's Non-Disclosure Agreement; Does the document state that Confidential Information shall only include technical information?

## Gold

For the purpose of this Agreement, Confidential Information shall also include, but is not necessarily limited to, any and all information, financial statements, legal documents, location of the practice(s), and identity of the Vendor, the fact that the practices are available for purchase, business records and plans, patient lists and records, employee lists and records, trade secrets, operations, technical information, pricing structure, costs, marketing strategies and market research and patient demographics pertaining to the professional practice(s) for which I am inquiring.

## RAG Answer

No. The ROI Corporation Non-Disclosure Agreement does **not** state that Confidential Information shall only include technical information.

It defines “Confidential Information” broadly and expressly says it includes, but is **not necessarily limited to**, many types of information, such as financial statements, legal documents, business records, patient lists, employee lists, trade secrets, operations, **technical information**, pricing structure, costs, marketing strategies, and other proprietary information. Technical information is only one example among many.
