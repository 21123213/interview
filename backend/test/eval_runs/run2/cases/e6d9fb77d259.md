## Query

Consider the Non-Disclosure Agreement between Artop and Inno; Does the document permit the Receiving Party to retain some Confidential Information even after its return or destruction?

## Gold

Nevertheless, the party possessing the confidential information may keep one piece of the duplicates of the documents or materials described above only for the purpose enshrined in Article 4 hereunder, without breaching other provisions of this agreement.

## RAG Answer

Yes. Under the NDA between Artop and Inno, the Receiving Party may retain a limited amount of Confidential Information after the required return/destruction.

Specifically, when the cooperative program ceases or a party quits, the party possessing the Confidential Information must destroy or return all Confidential Information and duplicates within five working days or upon written request. However, it may keep **one piece of the duplicates** of the documents or materials, **only for the purpose stated in Article 4**, and without breaching other provisions of the agreement.
