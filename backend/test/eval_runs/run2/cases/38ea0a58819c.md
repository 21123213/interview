## Query

Consider the Non-Disclosure Agreement between Dollar General and Portfolio Entity; Does the document allow the Receiving Party to acquire information similar to the Confidential Information from a third party?

## Gold

The Confidential Materials shall not include information that:
(iii) is disclosed to Buyer by a third party on a non-confidential basis; or

## RAG Answer

Yes, but only in a limited sense. The NDA does not expressly say the Receiving Party (Buyer) may obtain “similar” Confidential Information from a third party. However, Section 2 (“Excluded Information”) states that Confidential Materials do **not** include information that **“is disclosed to Buyer by a third party on a non-confidential basis.”**

Therefore, if a third party provides the information to Buyer on a non-confidential basis, that information is excluded from the confidentiality obligations under the agreement. If the third-party information is itself confidential, or was obtained using the Confidential Materials, the document does not permit that treatment.
