## Query

Consider Kenway's Non-Disclosure Agreement; Does the document permit the Receiving Party to create a copy of some Confidential Information under certain circumstances?

## Gold

The Recipient shall keep the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information confidential and, except with the prior written consent of the Disclosing Party, shall:
(c) not copy, reduce to writing or otherwise record the Confidential Information except as strictly necessary for the Purpose (and any such copies, reductions to writing and records shall be the property of the Disclosing Party);

## RAG Answer

Yes. Under Kenway’s NDA, the Recipient is generally prohibited from copying Confidential Information, but it may make a copy if doing so is **strictly necessary for the Purpose**.

Specifically, Clause 2.1(c) states that the Recipient shall **not copy, reduce to writing or otherwise record the Confidential Information except as strictly necessary for the Purpose**. Any such copies are also stated to be the **property of the Disclosing Party**, and Clause 2.1(f) requires the Recipient to keep a written record of any copy made.
