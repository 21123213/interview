## Query

Consider INFOMAGNET's Non-Disclosure Agreement; Does the document allow verbally conveyed information to be considered as Confidential Information?

## Gold

(c) "Confidential Materials" shall mean all tangible materials containing Confidential Information, including without limitation written or printed documents and computer disks or tapes, whether machine or user readable.
The terms "residuals" means information in non-tangible form, which may be retained by persons who have access to the Confidential Information, including ideas, concepts, know-how or techniques contained therein. Neither party shall have any obligation to limit or restrict the assignment of such persons or to pay royalties for any work resulting from the use of residuals.

## RAG Answer

Yes. The INFOMAGNET NDA does not limit “Confidential Information” to written or tangible materials.

Under the definition, “Confidential Information” means non-public information that the Disclosing Party either:

1. designates as confidential, or  
2. ought to be treated as confidential under the circumstances surrounding disclosure.

Therefore, information conveyed verbally can be considered Confidential Information if it meets that definition. The NDA separately defines “Confidential Materials” as tangible materials containing Confidential Information, but that does not exclude oral disclosures from being Confidential Information itself.
