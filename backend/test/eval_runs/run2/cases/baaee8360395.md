## Query

Consider Kerber's Non-Disclosure Agreement; Does the document include a clause that prohibits the Receiving Party from soliciting some of the Disclosing Party's representatives?

## Gold

Neither party shall not, and it shall ensure that any of its affiliates do not, for a period of five years from the date of this Agreement, without the prior written consent of the other party, induce any employee employed by the other party to leave such employ or offer to employ or employ such employee.

## RAG Answer

Yes. The Kerber Non-Disclosure Agreement includes a mutual non-solicitation clause. It states that neither party shall, and each party shall ensure its affiliates do not, for a period of five years from the date of the Agreement, without the other party’s prior written consent, induce any employee employed by the other party to leave that employment or offer to employ or employ that employee.

This would prohibit the Receiving Party from soliciting employees of the Disclosing Party. The clause specifically refers to “employees,” not all “representatives” generally.
