## Query

Consider LiiON's Non-Disclosure Agreement; Does the document require the Receiving Party to notify the Disclosing Party if they are required by law, regulation, or judicial process to disclose any Confidential Information?

## Gold

There shall be no restrictions under this Agree-ment with respect to any portion of the Confidential Information which:
(g) is requested or required to be disclosed by court order, government agency action or other legal process. In such event, Receiving Party shall, to the extent permissi-ble under applicable law, notify Disclosing Party of any such request in sufficient time to enable Disclosing Party to contest or prevent such disclosure or seek entry of a an appro-priate protective order.

## RAG Answer

Yes. Under Section 3(g) of the LiiON NDA, if Confidential Information is requested or required to be disclosed by court order, government agency action, or other legal process, the Receiving Party must, to the extent permissible under applicable law, notify the Disclosing Party in sufficient time to allow the Disclosing Party to contest or prevent the disclosure or seek an appropriate protective order.
