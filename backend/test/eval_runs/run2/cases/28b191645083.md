## Query

Consider the Non-Disclosure Agreement between Sqiller and Beta Testers; Does the document restrict the use of Confidential Information to the purposes stated in the Agreement?

## Gold

3.3. Authorized Use. Confidential Information shall be used exclusively for (i) discussions and/or negotiations, (ii) performance and/or enforcement, (iii) termination and/or disputes, of or relating to the Purpose, and shall not be used for any other purpose.

## RAG Answer

Yes. The agreement restricts the use of Confidential Information to the purposes stated in the agreement.

Specifically, Clause 3.3 (“Authorized Use”) states that Confidential Information must be used exclusively for:

1. discussions and/or negotiations;
2. performance and/or enforcement; and
3. termination and/or disputes,

in each case relating to the “Purpose.” It also expressly provides that the Confidential Information “shall not be used for any other purpose.”

The “Purpose” is defined in the agreement as contemplating, negotiating, executing, or already having entered into one or more agreements relating to testing computer software between the parties.
