## Query

Consider NCDG's Non-Disclosure Agreement; Does the document require the Receiving Party to notify the Disclosing Party if they are required by law, regulation, or judicial process to disclose any Confidential Information?

## Gold

4.Notwithstanding Clause 2.1, the Receiving Party shall not be prevented from disclosing Confidential Information, where (i) such disclosure is in response to a valid order of a court or any other governmental body having jurisdiction over this Agreement or (ii) such disclosure is otherwise required by law, provided that the Receiving Party, to the extent possible, has first given prior written notice to the Disclosing Party and made reasonable efforts to protect the Confidential Information in connection with such disclosure.

## RAG Answer

Yes. Under NCDG’s Non-Disclosure Agreement, if the Receiving Party is required to disclose Confidential Information by a valid court order, governmental order, or otherwise by law, it is not prevented from making that disclosure, but only if, **to the extent possible**, it **first gives prior written notice to the Disclosing Party** and makes **reasonable efforts to protect the Confidential Information** in connection with the disclosure.
