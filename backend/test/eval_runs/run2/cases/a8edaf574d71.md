## Query

Consider JB Machine LLC's Non-Disclosure Agreement; Does the document allow the Receiving Party to acquire information similar to the Confidential Information from a third party?

## Gold

The Term “Confidential information” does NOT include:
- Information rightfully received by the Recipient from a Third party without any duty of confidentiality

## RAG Answer

Yes. The NDA excludes from “Confidential Information” any information that the Receiving Party “rightfully received by the Recipient from a Third party without any duty of confidentiality.”

So the document permits the Receiving Party to have information similar to the Owner’s Confidential Information if it was lawfully obtained from a third party and that third party did not impose a confidentiality obligation. If the information was not rightfully received, or was received under a duty of confidentiality, the exclusion would not apply.
