## Query

Consider SWA's Non-Disclosure Agreement; Does the document permit the Receiving Party to retain some Confidential Information even after its return or destruction?

## Gold

6. The Receiving Party shall immediately upon request by the Disclosing party deliver back to the Disclosing Party all Confidential information disclosed to the Receiving party, including all copies(if any) mode under above clauses.

## RAG Answer

不可以。根据 SWA 的 Model NDA 片段：

- 第 6 条要求 Receiving Party 在 Disclosing Party 请求时，**立即返还全部 Confidential Information**，包括根据前述条款制作的**所有副本**。
- 第 5 条禁止 Receiving Party 未经 Disclosing Party 书面同意以任何方式复制、存储 Confidential Information，包括存入计算机或电子系统。
- 文档中没有允许在返还或销毁后保留部分 Confidential Information 的例外条款，例如存档副本或依法保留副本。

因此，除非获得披露方书面同意，接收方不得保留；且即便经同意制作了副本，在披露方请求返还时仍须返还。
