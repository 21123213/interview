## Query

Consider LiiON's Non-Disclosure Agreement; Does the document mention that some obligations of the Agreement may survive the termination of the Agreement?

## Gold

The restrictions and obligations set forth in this Agreement shall survive any expiration or termination for five years from the date of such expiration or termination with respect to Confidential Information disclosed under this Agreement prior to the date of such expiration or termination.

## RAG Answer

Yes. The LiiON Non-Disclosure Agreement expressly provides that certain obligations may survive termination or expiration.

Section 7(a) states:

> “The restrictions and obligations set forth in this Agreement shall survive any expiration or termination for five years from the date of such expiration or termination with respect to Confidential Information disclosed under this Agreement prior to the date of such expiration or termination.”

So, the Agreement says that the confidentiality restrictions and obligations continue for **five years after expiration or termination**, with respect to Confidential Information disclosed before that date.
