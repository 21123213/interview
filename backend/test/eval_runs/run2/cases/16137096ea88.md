## Query

Consider INFOMAGNET's Non-Disclosure Agreement; Does the document allow the Receiving Party to acquire information similar to the Confidential Information from a third party?

## Gold

(b) Confidential Information shall not include any information that: (i) is or subsequently becomes publicly available without Receiving Party's breach of any obligation owed to the Disclosing Party; (ii) became known to the Receiving Party prior to Disclosing Party's disclosure of such information to Receiving Party; (iii) became known to Receiving Party from a source other than Disclosing Party other than by breach of an obligation of confidentiality owed to Disclosing Party; or (iv) is independently developed or acquired legitimately by Receiving Party.
(b) The terms of confidentiality under this Agreement shall not be construed to limit either party's right to independently develop or acquire products without use of the other party's Confidential Information.

## RAG Answer

Yes.

The INFOMAGNET NDA allows the Receiving Party to acquire information similar to Confidential Information from a third party, provided it does not improperly use or disclose the Disclosing Party’s Confidential Information.

Specifically, the Agreement excludes from “Confidential Information” any information that:

- became known to the Receiving Party from a source other than the Disclosing Party, other than by breach of an obligation of confidentiality owed to the Disclosing Party; or  
- is independently developed or acquired legitimately by the Receiving Party.

It also states that the confidentiality terms do not limit either party’s right to independently develop or acquire products without use of the other party’s Confidential Information.

So, the Receiving Party may acquire similar information from a third party if the acquisition is legitimate, does not involve use of the Disclosing Party’s Confidential Information, and does not result from a breach of confidentiality owed to the Disclosing Party.
