## Query

Consider New York City Bar Association's Non-Disclosure Agreement; Does the document indicate that the Agreement does not grant the Receiving Party any rights to the Confidential Information?

## Gold

Buyer agrees that Buyer and its Representatives shall (i) use the Evaluation Material solely for the purpose of evaluating, negotiating and consummating the Transaction (or other legal, audit or compliance purposes permitted by this Agreement);13
All Evaluation Material is and shall remain property of the Company. Buyer acknowledges and agrees that none of the Company nor any of its Representatives grants any license to or other property right or interest in, by implication or otherwise, any copyright, patent, trademark, mask work, database or other intellectual or intangible property or proprietary information disclosed, embodied, fixed, comprised or contained in any Evaluation Material.

## RAG Answer

Yes. The document indicates that the Agreement does not grant the receiving party rights to the confidential information.

In this model NDA, the receiving party is the **Buyer**, and the confidential information is generally referred to as **Evaluation Material**. Section 13(c), titled **“Ownership of Evaluation Material; No License,”** states that all Evaluation Material remains the property of the Company, and that the Company and its Representatives do **not** grant the Buyer any license or other property right or interest, by implication or otherwise, in any intellectual or proprietary information contained in the Evaluation Material.

However, Section 2(a) does permit the Buyer to use the Evaluation Material for a limited purpose: evaluating, negotiating, and consummating the contemplated transaction.
