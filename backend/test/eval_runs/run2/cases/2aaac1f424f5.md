## Query

Consider Seeed's Non-Disclosure Agreement; Does the document mention that some obligations of the Agreement may survive the termination of the Agreement?

## Gold

This Agreement shall be effective as of the date first written above and shall continue in full force and effect until the last disclosure of Confidential Information by one Party to another and shall survive the term of this Agreement for a period of two (2) years thereafter, provided, however that as to any item of Confidential Information that constitutes a trade secret under applicable law, the obligations of confidentiality contained herein shall continue for so long as allowed under applicable law.

## RAG Answer

Yes. Section 7 (“Term”) states that the Agreement “shall survive the term of this Agreement for a period of two (2) years thereafter,” and for Confidential Information constituting a trade secret, the confidentiality obligations “shall continue for so long as allowed under applicable law.”
