## Query

Consider JB Machine LLC's Non-Disclosure Agreement; Does the document allow the Receiving Party to independently develop information that is similar to the Confidential Information?

## Gold

The Term “Confidential information” does NOT include:
- Independently developed information on behalf of the Recipient

## RAG Answer

Yes. The NDA expressly excludes “Independently developed information on behalf of the Recipient” from the definition of “Confidential Information.” Therefore, the Receiving Party may independently develop information that is similar to the Confidential Information, provided it is not derived from or disclosed under the agreement.
