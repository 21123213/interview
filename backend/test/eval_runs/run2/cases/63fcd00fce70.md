## Query

Consider the Terms of Collaboration Agreement between McGill University and Mitacs; Does the document allow the Receiving Party to acquire information similar to the Confidential Information from a third party?

## Gold

Exceptions to Confidential Information: This Agreement does not apply to information that:
iii. was received by Recipient from a third party and Recipient was not aware that the third party had a duty of confidentiality to Discloser in respect of the information;

## RAG Answer

Yes, but only under a specific exception.

Under the Agreement’s definition of **Confidential Information**, the obligations do **not** apply to information that:

> “was received by Recipient from a third party and Recipient was not aware that the third party had a duty of confidentiality to Discloser in respect of the information.”

Therefore, if the Receiving Party/Recipient obtains information that is similar to or overlaps with Confidential Information from a third party, it may be outside the confidentiality restrictions **provided** the Recipient was not aware that the third party owed a duty of confidentiality to the Discloser. If the Recipient knew that the third party had such a duty, the exception would not apply.
