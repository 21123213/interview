## Query

Consider the M5-Systems' Non-Disclosure Agreement; Does the document allow the Receiving Party to share some Confidential Information with their employees?

## Gold

The Recipient will not disclose any Confidential Information to third parties except those directors, officers, employees, consultants and agents of Recipient who are required to have the information in order to carry out the purpose set forth in Section 1 of this Agreement.

## RAG Answer

Yes. Under Section 3, the Receiving Party may share Confidential Information with its employees only if those employees are required to have the information to carry out the purpose set forth in Section 1. The Receiving Party must also ensure that such employees sign a Non-Disclosure Agreement substantially similar to this Agreement, promptly notify the Discloser in writing of their names after they sign, and take reasonable measures to protect the Confidential Information.
