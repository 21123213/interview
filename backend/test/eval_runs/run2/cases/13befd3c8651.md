## Query

Consider SWA's Non-Disclosure Agreement; Does the document specify whether the Receiving Party is required to destroy or return Confidential Information upon the termination of the Agreement?

## Gold

6. The Receiving Party shall immediately upon request by the Disclosing party deliver back to the Disclosing Party all Confidential information disclosed to the Receiving party, including all copies(if any) mode under above clauses.

## RAG Answer

No. The SWA Model NDA does not specify that the Receiving Party must destroy or return Confidential Information automatically upon termination of the Agreement.

It only states in Clause 6 that the Receiving Party must “immediately upon request by the Disclosing Party” deliver back all Confidential Information, including all copies made under the Agreement. There is no express termination-triggered destruction or return obligation in the provided document.
