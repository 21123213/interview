## Query

Consider the Non-Disclosure Agreement between Artop and Inno; Does the document allow the Receiving Party to share some Confidential Information with their employees?

## Gold

Both parties shall strictly limit the access to the confidential information to their responsible representatives only for the purpose specified hereunder.

## RAG Answer

Yes, but only in a limited way.

The NDA does not expressly use the word “employees,” but it states that both parties must “strictly limit the access to the confidential information to their responsible representatives only for the purpose specified hereunder.”

Therefore, the Receiving Party may share Confidential Information with employees only if those employees qualify as “responsible representatives” and the access is necessary for the specified purpose. It may not share the information more broadly or provide copies to third parties without the other party’s written consent.
