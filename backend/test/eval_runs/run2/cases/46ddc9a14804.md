## Query

Consider INFOMAGNET's Non-Disclosure Agreement; Does the document restrict the use of Confidential Information to the purposes stated in the Agreement?

## Gold

Further, either party shall be free to use for any purpose the residuals resulting from access to or from work with such Confidential Information, provided that either party shall maintain the confidentiality of the Confidential Information as provided herein.

## RAG Answer

Yes. The INFOMAGNET Non-Disclosure Agreement generally restricts the use of Confidential Information to purposes stated in the Agreement.

Specifically, Section 2(c) provides that Confidential Information and Confidential Materials “may be disclosed, reproduced, summarised, or distributed only in pursuance of the Receiving Party's business relationship with Disclosing Party, and only as otherwise provided hereunder.”

The Agreement also limits disclosure to the Receiving Party’s employees or bona-fide consultants on a need-to-know basis, and restricts disclosure to third parties except as permitted, such as under a judicial or governmental order.

However, there is a residual information exception: the Agreement allows either party to use “residuals” resulting from access to Confidential Information for any purpose, provided the party still maintains the confidentiality of the Confidential Information as required under the Agreement.
