## Query

Consider Fintricity's Non-Disclosure Agreement; Does the document allow the Receiving Party to independently develop information that is similar to the Confidential Information?

## Gold

The obligations of confidentiality in Clause 3 above shall not apply:‐
(b) to any development made by the recipient which is independently developed by the recipient without access to or use of the disclosing party's Confidential Information.

## RAG Answer

Yes. The NDA expressly excludes from the confidentiality obligations “any development made by the recipient which is independently developed by the recipient without access to or use of the disclosing party’s Confidential Information.” Therefore, the Receiving Party may independently develop information that is similar to the Confidential Information, provided it does not access or use Fintricity’s Confidential Information in doing so.
