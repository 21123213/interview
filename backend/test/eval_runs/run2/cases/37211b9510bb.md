## Query

Consider FullStory's Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement; Does the document allow the Receiving Party to share some Confidential Information with their employees?

## Gold

Recipient will disclose Discloser’s Confidential Information only to those of Recipient’s employees, consultants, and contractors who have a “need to know” the information to assist Recipient with respect to the Purpose and who are legally bound by terms and conditions substantially similar to those terms and conditions applicable to Recipient under this Agreement.

## RAG Answer

Yes. Under Section 2, the Receiving Party may disclose Confidential Information only to its employees, consultants, and contractors who have a “need to know” the information to assist with the Agreement’s Purpose, and only if they are legally bound by confidentiality terms substantially similar to those in the Agreement.
