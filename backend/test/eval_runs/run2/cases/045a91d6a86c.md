## Query

Consider LTI's Two-Way Confidential Disclosure Agreement; Does the document require that all Confidential Information be expressly identified by the Disclosing Party?

## Gold

Where the Proprietary Information has not been or cannot be reduced to written form at the time of disclosure and such disclosure is made orally or visually, complete written summaries of all proprietary aspects of any such oral or visual disclosures shall have been delivered to the receiving Party within 20 calendar days of said oral or visual disclosures; provided, however, that Proprietary Information that is not marked or reduced to writing is protected under this Non-Disclosure Agreement if the context and manner in which such Proprietary Information is disclosed would indicate to a reasonable person familiar with the pharmaceutical industry that such Proprietary Information can be reasonably expected to be treated as confidential.

## RAG Answer

No. The LTI two-way CDA template does not require that **all** confidential/proprietary information be expressly identified by the disclosing party.

The agreement uses the term **“Proprietary Information.”** Section 2 generally requires that Proprietary Information be in writing, clearly identified at the time of disclosure, marked on each page with an appropriate legend, and delivered by letter of transmittal. For oral or visual disclosures, it also requires written summaries within 20 calendar days.

However, the same section contains an express exception: Proprietary Information that is **not marked or reduced to writing** is still protected if the context and manner of disclosure would indicate to a reasonable person familiar with the pharmaceutical industry that the information can reasonably be expected to be treated as confidential.

So, express identification/marking is the default requirement, but it is not absolute.
