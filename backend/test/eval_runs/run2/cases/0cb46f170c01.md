## Query

Consider ROI Corporation's Non-Disclosure Agreement; Does the document allow the Receiving Party to share some Confidential Information with third parties, including consultants, agents, and professional advisors?

## Gold

The Receiving Party shall take all practical steps to keep the Disclosing Party’s Confidential Information confidential and shall restrict access to the Confidential Information to those agents and professional advisers to whom disclosure is necessary in pursuit of the Approved Purpose.

## RAG Answer

Yes, but only in a limited way.

The NDA permits the Receiving Party to restrict access to Confidential Information to “those agents and professional advisers to whom disclosure is necessary in pursuit of the Approved Purpose.” The Receiving Party must also inform such employees, agents, and professional advisers of the Agreement’s provisions and ensure their compliance.

The document does not explicitly mention “consultants.” If a consultant qualifies as an agent or professional adviser, disclosure may be permitted under the same condition that it is necessary for the Approved Purpose. Otherwise, the NDA does not expressly allow sharing with consultants.
