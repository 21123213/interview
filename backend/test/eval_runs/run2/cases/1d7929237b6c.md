## Query

Consider FullStory's Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement; Does the document permit the Receiving Party to create a copy of some Confidential Information under certain circumstances?

## Gold

Recipient agrees not to copy or reverse engineer, or attempt to derive the composition or underlying information, structure or ideas of any Confidential Information.

## RAG Answer

Yes, but only by exception.

Section 2 generally prohibits the Recipient (Receiving Party) from copying Confidential Information: “Recipient agrees not to copy or reverse engineer … any Confidential Information.”

However, Section 3 states that the Recipient’s obligations under Section 2 do not apply to Confidential Information that the Recipient can document was:

- in the public domain;
- already rightfully in the Recipient’s possession free of confidence;
- independently developed without reference to the Discloser’s Confidential Information; or
- communicated by the Discloser to an unaffiliated third party free of confidence.

For such excepted information, the Section 2 no-copy restriction does not apply. The document does not otherwise grant a general right to copy, and Section 7 still requires return of tangible Confidential Information upon request or termination and prohibits retention thereafter.
