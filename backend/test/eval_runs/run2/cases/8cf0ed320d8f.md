## Query

Consider JBF's Non-Disclosure Agreement; Does the document require that all Confidential Information be expressly identified by the Disclosing Party?

## Gold

This Agreement shall apply to all Confidential Information regardless of its form or medium, whether conveyed orally, visually, electronically or in writing, and whether or not it is designated as "confidential".

## RAG Answer

No.

JBF’s Non-Disclosure Agreement does **not** require the Disclosing Party to expressly identify or mark all Confidential Information.

The Agreement defines “Confidential Information” broadly as **any and all information** provided by or on behalf of the Disclosing Party that relates to the Purpose, regardless of form or format. It further states in Section 2 that the Agreement applies to all Confidential Information **whether conveyed orally, visually, electronically or in writing, and whether or not it is designated as “confidential.”**
