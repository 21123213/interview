## Query

Consider the Non-Disclosure Agreement between ON Semiconductor and Industry Analysts; Does the document state that Confidential Information shall only include technical information?

## Gold

Confidential Information includes, without limitation, any specification, layout, design, drawing, formula, technique, algorithm, know-how, sample product, test data, information related to engineering, manufacturing, sales, marketing, management or quality control, financial information or other information related to the business operations of the Discloser.

## RAG Answer

No. The document does **not** state that Confidential Information shall only include technical information.

Under Section 1 (“Definition”), Confidential Information means **any non-public information** disclosed by either party and identified as confidential or proprietary. It expressly includes, without limitation, technical information such as specifications, designs, formulas, algorithms, know-how, test data, and engineering/manufacturing information, **but also** information related to sales, marketing, management, quality control, financial information, and other business operations.
