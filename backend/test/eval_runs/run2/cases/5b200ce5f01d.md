## Query

Consider New York City Bar Association's Non-Disclosure Agreement; Does the document state that Confidential Information shall only include technical information?

## Gold

(b) “Evaluation Material” means any information or data concerning the Company or any of its Affiliates, whether in oral, visual, written, electronic or other form, that is disclosed to Buyer or any of its Representatives [before the date hereof,]6 now or in the future by or on behalf of the Company or any of its Representatives, together with all notes, memoranda, forecasts, summaries, analyses, compilations and other writings relating thereto that are prepared by or on behalf of Buyer or any of its Representatives to the extent that they use, contain, reflect or are derived from or incorporate, in whole or in part, any such information or data.

## RAG Answer

No. The document does not state that Confidential Information shall only include technical information.

In the provided excerpts, the confidentiality obligations are framed around **“Evaluation Material”** and **“Discussions Disclosure,”** not narrowly defined “Confidential Information.” “Evaluation Material” is defined broadly as **any information or data concerning the Company or its Affiliates, whether in oral, visual, written, electronic or other form**, together with related notes, memoranda, forecasts, summaries, analyses, compilations, etc. It also expressly excludes certain categories such as independently developed information, publicly available information, information received from a non-confidential source, or information already in Buyer’s possession.

The document also restricts “Discussions Disclosure,” which covers facts such as the existence of negotiations, the existence or contents of the agreement, due diligence activity, and transaction terms. Therefore, the confidentiality scope is not limited to technical information.
