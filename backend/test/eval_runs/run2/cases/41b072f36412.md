## Query

Consider LTI's Two-Way Confidential Disclosure Agreement; Does the document require the Receiving Party to notify the Disclosing Party if they are required by law, regulation, or judicial process to disclose any Confidential Information?

## Gold

If receiving Party is required by applicable law, or by process issued in connection with a judicial or administrative proceeding, to disclose any of disclosing Party’s Proprietary Information, receiving Party shall immediately notify disclosing Party of such legal requirement or the issuance of such process so disclosing Party has the opportunity to seek a protective order.

## RAG Answer

是的。该协议要求接收方在因适用法律，或司法/行政程序相关命令而被要求披露披露方的“Proprietary Information”（可理解为机密/专有信息）时，**立即通知披露方**，以便披露方有机会寻求保护令；若披露方未能取得保护令，接收方只能披露依法必须披露的信息。
