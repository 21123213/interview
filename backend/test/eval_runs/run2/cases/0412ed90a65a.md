## Query

Consider Ingram's Non-Disclosure Agreement; Does the document allow the Receiving Party to acquire information similar to the Confidential Information from a third party?

## Gold

This shall not, however, prevent Recipient from disclosing to others or using in any manner Confidential Information:
(ii) which has been furnished or made known to the undersigned by third parties as a matter of right without restriction of disclosure, or;

## RAG Answer

Yes. The NDA expressly permits the Receiving Party to disclose or use information that “has been furnished or made known to the undersigned by third parties as a matter of right without restriction of disclosure.”  

However, this only applies to information actually obtained from a third party without disclosure restrictions; it does not create a general right to acquire merely “similar” information.
