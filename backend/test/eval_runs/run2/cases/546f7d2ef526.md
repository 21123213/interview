## Query

Consider the Non-Disclosure Agreement between ON Semiconductor and Industry Analysts; Does the document require the Receiving Party to notify the Disclosing Party if they are required by law, regulation, or judicial process to disclose any Confidential Information?

## Gold

This Agreement imposes no obligation upon Recipient with respect to Confidential Information which is:
(e) is required to be disclosed pursuant to the order of a court of competent jurisdiction; or otherwise required to be disclosed by law through no act of the Recipient, provided, however, that the Recipient has notified the Discloser upon learning of the possibility that disclosure could be required pursuant to any such law or legal order and has given the Discloser a reasonable opportunity to contest or limit the scope of such required disclosure and has cooperated with the Discloser toward this end.

## RAG Answer

Yes.

Under the Agreement’s Exceptions clause, if the Receiving Party (Recipient) is required to disclose Confidential Information pursuant to a court order or otherwise by law, the Agreement conditions that exception on the Recipient having:

- notified the Discloser upon learning of the possibility that disclosure could be required;
- given the Discloser a reasonable opportunity to contest or limit the scope of the required disclosure; and
- cooperated with the Discloser toward that end.
