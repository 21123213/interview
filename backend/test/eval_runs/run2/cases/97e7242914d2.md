## Query

Consider the Terms of Collaboration Agreement between McGill University and Mitacs; Does the document mention that some obligations of the Agreement may survive the termination of the Agreement?

## Gold

The obligation to safeguard Confidential Information shall continue for a period of two (2) years from the date the internship/fellowship terminates or expires.
19. SURVIVAL: Notwithstanding the foregoing, the provisions of Section 3.2 (the academic and research license); Section 3.4 (Publication); Section 4 (Indemnification); Section 7 (Non-Disclosure); and Section 11 (Warranties) and will survive the end or other termination of the Project.

## RAG Answer

Yes. Section 19 (“Survival”) states that certain provisions survive the end or other termination of the Project, including:

- Section 3.2 (academic and research license);
- Section 3.4 (Publication);
- Section 4 (Indemnification);
- Section 7 (Non-Disclosure); and
- Section 11 (Warranties).

Additionally, Section 7.2 provides that the obligation to safeguard Confidential Information continues for two years after the internship/fellowship terminates or expires.
