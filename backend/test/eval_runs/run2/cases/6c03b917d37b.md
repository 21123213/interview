## Query

Consider LiiON's Non-Disclosure Agreement; Does the document restrict the use of Confidential Information to the purposes stated in the Agreement?

## Gold

(a) Receiving Party agrees that it will only use Disclosing Party's Confidential Information to the extent necessary for the Purpose.

## RAG Answer

Yes. The LiiON Non-Disclosure Agreement restricts the use of Confidential Information to the stated “Purpose.”

Specifically, Section 2(a) states that the Receiving Party “will only use Disclosing Party's Confidential Information to the extent necessary for the Purpose.” The “Purpose” is defined in Section 1(e) as:

1. discussing, assessing, or evaluating a party’s interest in establishing or furthering a current or future business relationship between the parties; and  
2. to the extent the NDA is incorporated by reference into another agreement between the parties, achieving the objectives of that agreement.

The Agreement also prohibits the Receiving Party from using Confidential Information “for its own benefit,” except as provided in the Agreement.
