## Query

## RAG Answer

No. Based on the provided fragments, the Agreement does not expressly permit the Receiving Party to retain Confidential Information after the required return or destruction.

- Section 5.9 also states that promptly after termination, both parties must return or destroy **all materials** provided under the Agreement.

Therefore, the document does not appear to allow retention of Confidential Information after return/destruction.
