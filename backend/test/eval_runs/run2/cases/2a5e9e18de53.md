## Query

Consider JBF's Non-Disclosure Agreement; Does the document permit the Receiving Party to create a copy of some Confidential Information under certain circumstances?

## Gold

6. The Confidential Information shall not be copied or reproduced in any form or stored in a retrieval system or database by the Receiving Party without the prior consent of the Disclosing Party except for such copies and storage as may reasonably be required internally by the Receiving Party for the Purpose.

## RAG Answer

Yes. Under Section 6 of the JBF Non-Disclosure Agreement, the Receiving Party may create copies of Confidential Information only in limited circumstances:

1. **With the Disclosing Party’s prior consent**, or  
2. **Internally, where copies and storage are reasonably required for the Purpose**.

All permitted copies must contain the same proprietary notices that appear on the original.
