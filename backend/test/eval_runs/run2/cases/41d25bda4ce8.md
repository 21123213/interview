## RAG Answer

Yes. Under the Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement, verbally conveyed information can be considered Confidential Information.

Section 2(a) defines “Confidential Information” broadly to include information in “written, oral, visually, electronic or another format,” including oral communications.

However, Section 2(b) adds a specific protection requirement for oral or visual information: it must be identified as Confidential Information at the time of disclosure and confirmed in writing as Confidential Information within fourteen (14) days after disclosure. Alternatively, information may still be protected if, given its nature or the circumstances of disclosure, it reasonably should be considered Confidential Information.
