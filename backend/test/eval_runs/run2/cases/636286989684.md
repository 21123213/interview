## Query

6.2 Termination of this Agreement shall not affect any accrued rights or remedies to which either party is entitled.

## RAG Answer

Yes. The Non-Disclosure Agreement between IGC and LSE expressly states that some obligations may survive termination.

> “The obligations of each party shall, notwithstanding any earlier termination of negotiations or discussions between the parties in relation to the Purpose, continue for a period of two (2) years from the termination of this Agreement.”

Clause 6.2 also states:

> “Termination of this Agreement shall not affect any accrued rights or remedies to which either party is entitled.”

Additionally, Clause 3.1(c) provides that:

> “The provisions of this Agreement shall continue to apply to any documents and materials retained by the Recipient.”

Therefore, the document clearly contemplates that certain obligations and rights continue after termination of the Agreement.
