## Query

Consider the M5-Systems' Non-Disclosure Agreement; Does the document allow the Receiving Party to share some Confidential Information with third parties, including consultants, agents, and professional advisors?

## Gold

The Recipient will not disclose any Confidential Information to third parties except those directors, officers, employees, consultants and agents of Recipient who are required to have the information in order to carry out the purpose set forth in Section 1 of this Agreement.

## RAG Answer

Yes, but only in a limited way.

Under Section 3 of the NDA, the Receiving Party is generally prohibited from disclosing Confidential Information to third parties, **except** to the Receiving Party’s **directors, officers, employees, consultants, and agents** who are required to have the information to carry out the purpose stated in the Agreement.

However, the document **does not expressly mention “professional advisors.”** Therefore, sharing with professional advisors is not clearly permitted unless they qualify as “agents” or another listed category. Any permitted disclosure must also be subject to confidentiality obligations substantially similar to the NDA, and the Receiving Party must promptly notify the Discloser of the names of persons who signed such agreements.
