## Query

Consider Kenway's Non-Disclosure Agreement; Does the document allow the Receiving Party to share some Confidential Information with third parties, including consultants, agents, and professional advisors?

## Gold

“Representative”: means employees, agents, officers, advisers and other representatives of the Recipient.
The Recipient may disclose the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information to those of its Representatives who need to know this Confidential Information for the Purpose, provided that:

## RAG Answer

Yes. Under the Kenway NDA, the Receiving Party may share some Confidential Information with certain third parties who are its **Representatives**, provided they **need to know** the information for the **Purpose**.

“Representative” is defined to include **employees, agents, officers, advisers and other representatives**, so consultants and professional advisors can fall within this category. However, disclosure is not unlimited: the Receiving Party must:

- inform them that the information is confidential;
- ensure they comply with the NDA as if they were the Receiving Party;
- keep a written record of such Representatives; and
- remain liable for any Representative’s failure to comply.

General disclosure to unrelated third parties is otherwise prohibited unless expressly permitted by the Agreement or required by law.
