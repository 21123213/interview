## Query

Consider the Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement between The Knights of Unity; Does the document allow the Receiving Party to share some Confidential Information with third parties, including consultants, agents, and professional advisors?

## Gold

4. Confidential Information may be disclosed by the receiving party to its employees, directors, officers, advisors, attorneys, auditors and agents only on a need-to-know basis in connection with the Opportunity, and each employee, director, officer, advisor, attorney, auditor or agent to whom Confidential Information is disclosed shall treat such Confidential Information in a manner consistent with this Agreement.

## RAG Answer

Yes. Under the Agreement, the Receiving Party may disclose Confidential Information to certain third parties, including its advisors, attorneys, auditors, and agents, but only on a need-to-know basis in connection with the Opportunity. Those recipients must treat the information consistently with the Agreement, and the Receiving Party remains liable for their acts and omissions.

The Agreement does not expressly list “consultants,” but it does allow disclosure to “advisors,” which may cover professional advisors depending on interpretation. General disclosure to third parties is otherwise prohibited except as permitted by the Agreement.
