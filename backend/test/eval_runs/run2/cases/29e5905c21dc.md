## Query

Consider Employer's Non-Disclosure Agreement; Does the document require that all Confidential Information be expressly identified by the Disclosing Party?

## Gold

b) With respect to any tangible information that a Disclosing Party would like to be treated as Confidential Information under this Agreement, the Disclosing Party shall mark such information as “Confidential” prior to disclosing it to the Recipient.
c) With respect to any oral or visual communication or other intangible information which a Disclosing Party would like to be treated as Confidential Information under this Agreement, the Disclosing Party shall notify Recipient of such fact at the time of disclosure and within fifteen (15) days thereafter, Disclosing Party shall send Recipient a written memorandum outlining the information deemed to be Confidential Information. Such memorandum shall be marked “Confidential.

## RAG Answer

Yes. Under Section 1, the Agreement defines “Confidential Information” as information that is “marked in accordance with this Section 1.” Specifically:

- Tangible information must be marked “Confidential” by the Disclosing Party before disclosure.
- Oral, visual, or other intangible information must be identified as confidential at the time of disclosure, and the Disclosing Party must send a written memorandum within 15 days outlining the information deemed confidential, marked “Confidential.”

Therefore, information intended to be treated as Confidential Information must be expressly identified or marked by the Disclosing Party.
