## Query

Consider FullStory's Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement; Does the document allow the Receiving Party to acquire information similar to the Confidential Information from a third party?

## Gold

3. Recipient’s obligations under Section 2 will not apply to any of Discloser’s Confidential Information that Recipient can document:
(d) was communicated by Discloser to an unaffiliated third party free of any obligation of confidence.

## RAG Answer

Conditional yes.

The Agreement does not expressly give the Receiving Party a general right to acquire similar information from a third party. However, the Receiving Party’s confidentiality obligations under Section 2 do **not** apply to Confidential Information that the Receiving Party can document was:

- communicated by the Discloser to an unaffiliated third party free of any obligation of confidence, or  
- otherwise rightfully in the Receiving Party’s possession free of confidence, or  
- independently developed without reference to the Confidential Information.

So, if the third-party information falls within one of these documented exceptions, the Receiving Party may use it. Otherwise, the Receiving Party may not use or disclose the Confidential Information except for the permitted business-evaluation Purpose.
