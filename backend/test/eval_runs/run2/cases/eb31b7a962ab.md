## Query

Consider Seeed's Non-Disclosure Agreement; Does the document allow the Receiving Party to share some Confidential Information with third parties, including consultants, agents, and professional advisors?

## Gold

Any disclosure to any third party by each Party shall be subject to the prior consent of the other Party, which consent shall not be unreasonably withheld unless the Disclosing Party believe it might go against the purpose of this Agreement.
The Receiving Party shall use its best efforts to limit dissemination of Disclosing Party’s “Confidential Information” to such of its employees or sub-contractor(s) who have a need to know for the aforesaid evaluation and/or development purposes.

## RAG Answer

Yes, but only in a limited and conditional way.

Under Seeed’s NDA, the Receiving Party generally may not disclose Confidential Information to any person or entity except:

1. **Its own employees** who have a “need to know” and are bound by similar non-disclosure obligations; and  
2. **Other recipients approved in writing** by the relevant Party, provided those recipients first execute a confidentiality agreement in a form acceptable to the interested Party.

The NDA also states that **any disclosure to a third party is subject to the prior consent of the other Party**, which consent shall not be unreasonably withheld unless the Disclosing Party believes it would go against the purpose of the Agreement.

The document does **not explicitly list “consultants, agents, and professional advisors”** as permitted recipients. However, such third parties could potentially receive Confidential Information if they are approved in writing/consented to and are bound by an acceptable confidentiality agreement.
