## Query

Consider the Non-Disclosure Agreement between IBC and PMS; Does the document allow the Receiving Party to acquire information similar to the Confidential Information from a third party?

## Gold

Confidential Information shall not include any information, other than Personal Information, however designated, that:
(iii) became known to Vendor from a source other than IBC other than by the breach of an obligation of confidentiality owed to IBC; or

## RAG Answer

Yes. Under the agreement, the Receiving Party (Vendor) may obtain information from a source other than IBC, including a third party, provided that:

1. The information is **not Personal Information**; and  
2. The information did not become known to the Vendor **by breaching an obligation of confidentiality owed to IBC**.

In that case, the information is **excluded from “Confidential Information”** under the NDA.

The agreement also separately excludes information that the Vendor **independently develops without relying on IBC’s Confidential Information**.
