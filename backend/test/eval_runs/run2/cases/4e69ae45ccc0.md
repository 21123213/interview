## Query

Consider the Non-Disclosure Agreement between IBC and PMS; Does the document allow the Receiving Party to share some Confidential Information with third parties, including consultants, agents, and professional advisors?

## Gold

(c) The undersigned Vendor may disclose Confidential Information only to Vendor's employees and consultants on a need-to-know basis but in any event only if Vendor has executed written agreements with its employees and consultants that contain obligations that are no less restrictive than those contained in this Agreement and has informed such employees and consultants of the confidential nature of such information.

## RAG Answer

Yes, but only in a limited way.

Under Section 2(a)(ii), the Vendor may not disclose Confidential Information to third parties except as expressly provided in Sections 2(b) and 2(c). Section 2(c) permits disclosure only to the Vendor’s **employees and consultants**, on a **need-to-know basis**, and only if the Vendor has written agreements with them imposing confidentiality obligations no less restrictive than the Agreement and has informed them that the information is confidential.

The document does **not** expressly allow disclosure to “agents” or “professional advisors” generally, unless they qualify as the Vendor’s consultants under Section 2(c).
