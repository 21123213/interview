## Query

Consider Inaturals's Non-Disclosure Agreement; Does the document indicate that the Agreement does not grant the Receiving Party any rights to the Confidential Information?

## Gold

5. The parties are the owners or exclusive licensees of protected intellectual property, including but not limited to trademarks, patents and copyrights.

## RAG Answer

Yes, but only in a qualified sense. The NDA indicates that the Receiving Party is not granted rights to Confidential Information by stating that the Recipient may use it only for the purposes set forth in the Agreement (Section 1), and that no disclosure of information relating to intellectual property shall be deemed a transfer, assignment, grant, or waiver of any rights or privileges (Section 6).

However, the explicit “no rights granted” language is specifically tied to intellectual property-related disclosures, not a broad statement covering all Confidential Information.
