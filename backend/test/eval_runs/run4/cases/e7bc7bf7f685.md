## Query

## RAG Answer

Yes. TPI’s Non-Disclosure Agreement restricts the use of confidential/proprietary information to the purposes stated in the Agreement.

Specifically, the document states that “Proprietary Information” shall be held in strict confidence and “shall not be used by Receiving Party except for (i) the purposes described in this Agreement,” unless further use or disclosure is authorized or consented to in writing by the Disclosing Party, or unless disclosure is required under applicable law or governmental authority. It also permits disclosure only to employees or affiliated companies with a legitimate “need to know” and only for the purposes described in the Agreement.
