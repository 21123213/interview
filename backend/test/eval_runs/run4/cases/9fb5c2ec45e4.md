## Query

Consider tpi's Non-Disclosure Agreement; Does the document indicate that the Agreement does not grant the Receiving Party any rights to the Confidential Information?

## Gold

5. No rights or obligations other than those expressed and recited herein are to be implied from this Agreement. In particular, no licenses are hereby granted directly or indirectly under any patent, copyright, or trademark now held by or which may be obtained by, or which is licensable by Disclosing Party, including but not limited to, any license to make, use or sell any product embodying any Proprietary Information.
Disclosing Party hereto confers no right to Receiving Party to use in advertising, publicity, or otherwise any trademark or trade name of Disclosing Party, nor confers any authorization to Receiving Party to act as an agent on its behalf for any purpose.

## RAG Answer

Yes. The TPI Non-Disclosure Agreement indicates that it does not grant the Receiving Party rights to the Confidential/Proprietary Information beyond those expressly stated.

Specifically:

- **Section 5** states that **“No rights or obligations other than those expressed and recited herein are to be implied from this Agreement.”**
- It further states that **no licenses are granted directly or indirectly** under any patent, copyright, or trademark held or obtainable by the Disclosing Party, including **no license to make, use, or sell any product embodying Proprietary Information**.
- It also says the Disclosing Party **confers no right** to the Receiving Party to use its trademarks, trade names, or act as its agent.
- **Section 2** limits the Receiving Party’s use of Proprietary Information to **the purposes described in the Agreement** and requires strict confidentiality.
- **Section 4** confirms that nothing in the Agreement requires disclosure of Proprietary Information and requires return or destruction upon request.

So, the Agreement does **not grant the Receiving Party any general or IP-related rights** to the Confidential/Proprietary Information; it only permits limited use for the Agreement’s stated purposes under confidentiality obligations.
