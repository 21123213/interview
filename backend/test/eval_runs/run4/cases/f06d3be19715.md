## Query

Consider The Munt's Non-Disclosure Agreement; Does the document allow verbally conveyed information to be considered as Confidential Information?

## Gold

Confidential Information shall mean the following:
a) all such information, of any kind whatsoever (whether in oral, written or electronic form, and including, but not limited to, technical, commercial, financial, accounting, legal and administrative information) pertaining to the Sale of the Munt and the Sellers as may be provided to the Disclosee and their responsible managers, officers, employees, shareholders, members of the Board of Directors and advisors (including financial, legal and tax advisors and auditors) (“Representatives”), by the Sellers, their advisors or their representatives;

## RAG Answer

Yes. The document expressly defines Confidential Information as “all such information, of any kind whatsoever (whether in oral, written or electronic form…)” provided to the Disclosee or its Representatives by the Sellers, their advisors, or representatives.

Therefore, verbally conveyed information can be considered Confidential Information if it relates to the Sale of the Munt and the Sellers, subject to the stated exclusions, such as information already publicly available or already lawfully possessed on a non-confidential basis.
