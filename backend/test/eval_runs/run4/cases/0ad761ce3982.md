## Query

Consider the AGProjects' Non-Disclosure Agreement; Does the document permit the Receiving Party to create a copy of some Confidential Information under certain circumstances?

## Gold

Tangible forms of the CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION shall not be copied, in whole or in part, without the prior written consent of the DISCLOSING PARTY.

## RAG Answer

Yes. The NDA generally prohibits copying tangible forms of Confidential Information, but it permits the Receiving Party to create a copy—whether in whole or in part—if the Receiving Party obtains the Disclosing Party’s prior written consent.
