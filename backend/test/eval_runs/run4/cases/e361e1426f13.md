## Query

Consider tpi's Non-Disclosure Agreement; Does the document require the Receiving Party to notify the Disclosing Party if they are required by law, regulation, or judicial process to disclose any Confidential Information?

## Gold

2. Proprietary Information shall be held in strict confidence by Receiving Party and shall not be used by Receiving Party except for
(ii) the case where disclosure of the same is required under applicable law or by a governmental order, rule or regulation or by the regulations of any relevant stock exchange or other governmental authority (provided that the Receiving party shall give written notice of such required disclosure to the other party prior to the disclosure).

## RAG Answer

Yes. Under the TPI Non-Disclosure Agreement, if disclosure of Proprietary Information is required under applicable law or by a governmental order, rule, regulation, or the regulations of a relevant stock exchange or other governmental authority, the Receiving Party must give **written notice** of such required disclosure to the other Party **prior to the disclosure**.

The document does not expressly use the phrase “judicial process,” but a court order or similar governmental authority would generally fall within the “governmental order” requirement.
