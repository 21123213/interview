## Query

## Gold

## RAG Answer

No. The document does not state that Confidential Information shall only include technical information.

Under the Agreement, “Confidential Information” is defined broadly as information disclosed by the Disclosing Party or its Affiliates that is marked “Confidential” or “Proprietary” in writing/electronic form, or disclosed orally/visually and then confirmed in writing within ten working days. It also expressly includes personal data, and is not limited to technical information. Technical data is mentioned separately in the export-control clause, but not as the exclusive scope of Confidential Information.
