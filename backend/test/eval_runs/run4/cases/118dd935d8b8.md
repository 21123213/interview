## Query

Consider the Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement between Bosch and Automotive Service Solutions; Does the document state that Confidential Information shall only include technical information?

## Gold

Confidential Information includes, but is not limited to documents, drawings, models, apparatus, sketches, designs, schedules, product plans, marketing plans, technical procedures, manufacturing processes, software, prototypes, samples, methodologies, formulations, trade secrets, patent applications, know-how, experimental results, specifications and other business information.

## RAG Answer

No. The document does **not** state that Confidential Information shall only include technical information.

Under Section 1 (“Confidential Information”), Confidential Information means **all nonpublic information relating to the Subject Matter** disclosed by a party, and it expressly includes, but is not limited to, items such as:

- documents, drawings, models, apparatus, sketches, designs  
- schedules, product plans, marketing plans  
- technical procedures, manufacturing processes, software, prototypes, samples  
- methodologies, formulations, trade secrets, patent applications, know-how  
- experimental results, specifications  
- **other business information**

Therefore, Confidential Information is defined broadly and is not limited to technical information.
