## Query

Consider AfriGIS's Non-Disclosure Agreement; Does the document indicate that the Agreement does not grant the Receiving Party any rights to the Confidential Information?

## Gold

All Confidential Information disclosed by the Disclosing Party to the Receiving Party is acknowledged by the Receiving Party –
4.2 not to confer any rights to the Receiving Party of whatever nature in the Confidential Information.

## RAG Answer

Yes. The AfriGIS Non-Disclosure Agreement expressly states under clause **4 TITLE** that all Confidential Information disclosed by the Disclosing Party is acknowledged by the Receiving Party:

- **4.1** to be proprietary to the Disclosing Party; and  
- **4.2** **not to confer any rights to the Receiving Party of whatever nature in the Confidential Information**.

Therefore, the document clearly indicates that the Agreement does not grant the Receiving Party any rights to the Confidential Information.
