## Query

Consider ADVANIDE's Non-Disclosure Agreement; Does the document allow the Receiving Party to share some Confidential Information with third parties, including consultants, agents, and professional advisors?

## Gold

2. Both Parties shall
II. keep the Information relating to the other party secret and confidential and not disclose any of it to any third person and only make it available to the Receiving Party´s executive board, directors, employees, advisers or subsidiaries and affiliates who need to know the same for the Purpose;

## RAG Answer

Yes, but only in a limited way.

Under the ADVANIDE NDA, the Receiving Party must not disclose the Confidential Information to “any third person,” except that it may make the Information available to the Receiving Party’s **executive board, directors, employees, advisers, or subsidiaries and affiliates** who **need to know it for the Business Purpose**. The Receiving Party must also ensure that its **employees and advisers** comply with the non-disclosure obligations.

Therefore, the document allows sharing with certain persons who can be characterized as **advisers**, which may include professional advisors. However, **consultants and agents are not expressly listed**; they would likely need to qualify as “advisers” and be bound by the confidentiality obligations before the Information could be shared with them.
