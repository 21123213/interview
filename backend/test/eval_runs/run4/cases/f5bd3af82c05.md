## Query

Consider UALR's Non-Disclosure Agreement; Does the document allow the Receiving Party to share some Confidential Information with their employees?

## Gold

Confidential Information shall only be disclosed to the Receiving Party’s employees and, even then, only to the extent that such employees have a specific need to know of the Confidential information, for the evaluation of the proposed transaction.

## RAG Answer

Yes. The document allows the Receiving Party to disclose Confidential Information to its employees, but only to those with a specific need to know it for evaluating the proposed transaction. Before receiving any Confidential Information, those employees must read the Non-Disclosure Agreement and acknowledge/agree to abide by the Receiving Party’s obligations under it.
