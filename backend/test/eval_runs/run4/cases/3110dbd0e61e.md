## Query

Consider ResConnect's Non-Disclosure Agreement; Does the document allow the Receiving Party to share some Confidential Information with their employees?

## Gold

Any Business Partner shall maintain the material as confidential and shall not publish, disclose or distribute all or any portion of it (or any copies of it) to any other person or permit any other person access to it, save for employees, consultants or agents of the Business Partner for purposes specifically related to Mandarin’s use of the material.

## RAG Answer

Yes, but only in a limited way.

Under the NDA, the Business Partner (the receiving party) may permit its **employees, consultants, or agents** to access the **Materials** for purposes specifically related to Mandarin’s use of the material (Clause 3.2). The document also contemplates that the parties’ respective officers and employees must keep confidential information confidential (Clause 2.1).

However, the NDA does **not** generally allow broad sharing of all Confidential Information with employees. Disclosure is restricted, and third-party disclosure is only permitted in specified circumstances, such as to accountants, attorneys, financial advisors, funders, or where required by law (Clause 2.4).
