## Query

Consider the Data Use and Non-Disclosure Agreement between MDCH Trauma Registry; Does the document specify whether the Receiving Party is required to destroy or return Confidential Information upon the termination of the Agreement?

## Gold

With regard to the data disclosed to MDHHS under this Agreement, the MDHHS agrees to:
7. Destroy all originals and copies of potentially identifiable information, in any format, in accordance with industry standards when no longer needed.

## RAG Answer

No. Based on the provided document excerpts, the Agreement does not explicitly state that the Receiving Party must destroy or return Confidential Information upon termination of the Agreement.

It does require the receiving party to:

- Destroy all originals and copies of potentially identifiable information, in any format, in accordance with industry standards **when no longer needed**; and
- Clean computer hard drives and portable storage media of data received under the Agreement before disposal.

However, there is no clear provision stating that these obligations are triggered specifically by termination, nor is there a requirement to return Confidential Information. The termination clause only states that the Agreement may be terminated upon sixty days’ written notice.
