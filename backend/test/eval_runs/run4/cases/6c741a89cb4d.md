## Query

Consider VELCO's Non-Disclosure Agreement; Does the document allow the Receiving Party to share some Confidential Information with third parties, including consultants, agents, and professional advisors?

## Gold

The foregoing notwithstanding, the Recipient may disclose classified information to its employees or contractors (hereafter Representatives) to the extent each such Representative has a need to know such information to conduct the work referenced in the Whereas clauses, and shall comply with Recipient’s obligations under this Agreement.

## RAG Answer

No—not broadly. VELCO’s NDA generally prohibits the Receiving Party from divulging Classified/Confidential Information to any third party without VELCO’s prior written consent.

It does allow limited disclosure to the Receiving Party’s **employees or contractors** (“Representatives”) if each has a need to know the information for the work described in the NDA and must comply with the NDA’s obligations. The document does **not expressly permit sharing with agents or professional advisors**. Consultants are not listed in the main permitted-disclosure clause, although the company NDA signature page requires the company to assume responsibility for employees’, contractors’, and consultants’ compliance.
