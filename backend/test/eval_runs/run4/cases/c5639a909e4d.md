## Query

Consider the Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement between AMC and Other Party; Does the document allow the Receiving Party to share some Confidential Information with third parties, including consultants, agents, and professional advisors?

## Gold

Each party may disclose the other party’s Confidential Information to its employees, contractors and Affiliates who have a legitimate “need to know,” have been advised of the obligations of confidentiality under this Agreement and are bound to obligations of confidentiality substantially similar to those set out in this Agreement.

## RAG Answer

Yes, but only in a limited way.

Under Section 3(a) of the Mutual NDA, the Receiving Party may disclose Confidential Information to its **employees, contractors, and Affiliates**, provided they have a legitimate “need to know,” have been advised of the confidentiality obligations, and are bound by obligations substantially similar to those in the Agreement.

However, the document does **not expressly list consultants, agents, or professional advisors** as permitted third-party recipients. Therefore, sharing with such third parties would generally require the Disclosing Party’s prior written consent, unless they qualify as permitted “contractors” or “Affiliates” and meet the stated confidentiality conditions.
