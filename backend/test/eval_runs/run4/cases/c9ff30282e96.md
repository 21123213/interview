## Query

Consider the Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement between Bosch and Automotive Service Solutions; Does the document indicate that the Agreement does not grant the Receiving Party any rights to the Confidential Information?

## Gold

Neither the execution of this Agreement nor the disclosure of any Confidential Information is construed as granting either expressly or by implication, estoppel or otherwise, any license or right to the Confidential Information or any intellectual property rights embodied therein.

## RAG Answer

Yes. The Agreement indicates that the Receiving Party (“Recipient”) is granted no rights to the Confidential Information.

Specifically, Section 10 (“NO LICENSE”) states that neither the execution of the Agreement nor the disclosure of Confidential Information is construed as granting “any license or right to the Confidential Information or any intellectual property rights embodied therein.” Section 9 (“OWNERSHIP OF CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION”) also confirms that the Disclosing Party or its licensors retain all rights in the Confidential Information.
