## Query

Consider the Non-Disclosure Agreement between CEII and NDA; Does the document include a clause that prevents the Receiving Party from disclosing the fact that the Agreement was agreed upon or negotiated?

## Gold

Except as may be required by applicable law, without the prior written consent of the respective Disclosing Party, the Receiving Party shall not:
(b) disclose to any person the fact that Confidential Information and/or CEII/CII have been made available to it; (c) confirm that any investigations, discussions or negotiations are taking place; or (d) disclose any of the terms or conditions with respect to same.

## RAG Answer

Yes, but only indirectly/limitedly.

The NDA includes a confidentiality restriction stating that, except as required by applicable law and without prior written consent, the Receiving Party shall not:

- “confirm that any investigations, discussions or negotiations are taking place”; or  
- “disclose any of the terms or conditions with respect to same.”

So the document prevents the Receiving Party from confirming or disclosing that negotiations/discussions are occurring and from disclosing related terms.

However, the provided clauses do not expressly state that the Receiving Party may not disclose the fact that the Agreement itself was agreed upon, signed, or reached. The closest prohibition is the restriction on confirming that discussions or negotiations are taking place.
