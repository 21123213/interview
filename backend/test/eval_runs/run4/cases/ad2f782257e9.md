For purposes of this Agreement, CEII shall mean: (i) all information designated as such by VELCO, whether furnished before or after the date hereof, whether oral, written or recorded/electronic, and regardless of the manner in which it is furnished; and
CI subject to this Agreement may be in intangible form, such as information communicated orally or by visual observation, or may be embodied in tangible form, such as a document.
