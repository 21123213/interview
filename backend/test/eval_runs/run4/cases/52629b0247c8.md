## Query

Consider tpi's Non-Disclosure Agreement; Does the document allow verbally conveyed information to be considered as Confidential Information?

## Gold

1. “Proprietary Information,” as used herein, shall mean any and all samples, formula, methods, know-how, technology, software, material, engineering data, specifications, sketches, drawings, schematics, designs, manufacturing processes, test results, compilations, and any other material, information, ideas, concepts or knowledge which a party (the “Disclosing Party”) furnishes to another party (the “Receiving Party”):
(ii) in oral or visual form that is identified as proprietary at the time of disclosure and is summarized and designated proprietary in a written memorandum delivered to Receiving Party within thirty (30) days of the disclosure.

## RAG Answer

Yes. Under TPI’s Non-Disclosure Agreement, “Proprietary Information” (i.e., confidential information) can include information conveyed orally or visually, but only if it is:

1. **identified as proprietary at the time of disclosure**, and  
2. **summarized and designated as proprietary in a written memorandum delivered to the receiving party within 30 days** of the disclosure.

So verbally conveyed information may be considered confidential, but it must meet those conditions.
