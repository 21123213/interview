## Query

Consider UALR's Non-Disclosure Agreement; Does the document require that all Confidential Information be expressly identified by the Disclosing Party?

## Gold

1. Confidential Information is defined as any information, whether written or verbal, of either party hereto, (Disclosing Party) which is disclosed to or observed by the other party (Receiving Party) in connection with or as a result of the evaluation of any possible transaction between the University of Arkansas at Little Rock and ______________________________ and which is, at the time of disclosure, marked as being Confidential or Proprietary, or is reasonably identifiable as confidential, proprietary information of the Disclosing Party pertaining to information in the areas of (subject matter): _______________________________________________ ___________________________.

## RAG Answer

No.

Under Article 1 of the UALR Standard Non-Disclosure Agreement, Confidential Information is not limited to information that is expressly marked or identified by the Disclosing Party. The document defines Confidential Information as information that, at the time of disclosure, is either:

1. **marked as “Confidential” or “Proprietary,”** or  
2. **reasonably identifiable as confidential or proprietary information** of the Disclosing Party in the specified subject-matter areas.

It also states that **Confidential Information disclosed verbally will also be included as proprietary**. Therefore, express identification or marking is not required for all Confidential Information.
