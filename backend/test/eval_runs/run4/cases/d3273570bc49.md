## Query

Consider NSK's Confidentiality Agreement for Suppliers; Does the document mention that some obligations of the Agreement may survive the termination of the Agreement?

## Gold

3.1. In consideration of the Disclosing Party disclosing Confidential Information to the Receiving Party, the Receiving Party undertakes that it shall:
a) keep the Confidential Information secret and confidential, using at least the same degree of care as its uses to protect its own confidential information but no less than a reasonable degree of care;
10.2. The rights and obligations contained in this Agreement shall continue in full force and effect for [two (2)] years after expiration of the Term or termination of this Agreement.

## RAG Answer

Yes. The document explicitly includes a survival provision.

Under Clause 10, titled **“TERM AND SURVIVAL OF CONFIDENTIALITY OBLIGATIONS,”** Clause 10.2 states that:

> “The rights and obligations contained in this Agreement shall continue in full force and effect for [two (2)] years after expiration of the Term or termination of this Agreement.”

Therefore, the Agreement does mention that certain rights and obligations may survive the termination of the Agreement, specifically for two years after the term expires or the Agreement is terminated.
