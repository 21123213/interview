## Query

Consider eHandshake's Non-Disclosure Agreement; Does the document mention that some obligations of the Agreement may survive the termination of the Agreement?

## Gold

6. If any of the Parties determine that they do not wish to proceed or continue with the Business Under Discussion, it will promptly advise the other Party of that decision in writing or by email. Upon receipt of such communication the Receiving Party shall destroy promptly all documents furnished and will not retain any copies, extracts or other reproductions in whole or in part of such Information. Notwithstanding the foregoing, the Receiving Party shall be entitled to retain that portion of the Information for legal, regulatory or internal compliance purposes, which will continue to be treated as confidential on the terms hereof.

## RAG Answer

Based on the provided excerpts, the eHandshake Non-Disclosure Agreement does **not** contain an explicit “survival of obligations after termination” clause.

However, Clause 6 states that if the Parties decide not to proceed or continue with the Business Under Discussion, the Receiving Party must destroy furnished documents, except that it may retain information for legal, regulatory, or internal compliance purposes, and that retained information “will continue to be treated as confidential on the terms hereof.” This suggests some confidentiality obligations may continue after the discussions end, but it is not a general statement that obligations survive termination of the Agreement.
