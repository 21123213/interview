## Query

Consider eHandshake's Non-Disclosure Agreement; Does the document allow the Receiving Party to acquire information similar to the Confidential Information from a third party?

## Gold

3. Information does not include, however, information which
(d) becomes available to the Receiving Party on a non-confidential basis after the date hereof from a third party which was not known by the Receiving Party to be subject to a confidentiality agreement with the Providing Party and which is not otherwise prohibited from transmitting the information to the Receiving Party.

## RAG Answer

Yes, but only in limited circumstances.

Under the eHandshake Non-Disclosure Agreement, the Receiving Party may acquire information from a third party if that information:

1. becomes available to the Receiving Party on a **non-confidential basis** after the date of the Agreement;
2. comes from a third party that the Receiving Party **does not know** is subject to a confidentiality agreement with the Providing Party; and
3. comes from a third party that is **not otherwise prohibited** from transmitting the information to the Receiving Party.

If these conditions are met, such information is excluded from the definition of confidential “Information” under the Agreement.
