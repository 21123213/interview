## Query

Consider the Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement between Bosch and Automotive Service Solutions; Does the document allow the Receiving Party to acquire information similar to the Confidential Information from a third party?

## Gold

Recipient shall have no obligation of confidentiality with respect to any information which:
5.3. is rightfully acquired from others who did not obtain it under obligation of confidentiality; or
Disclosing Party understands that Recipient may develop or have developed information internally, or receive or have received information from other parties that is similar to the Confidential Information.

## RAG Answer

Yes. The document acknowledges that the Receiving Party may receive information from other parties that is similar to the Confidential Information.

In particular, Section 13 states that the Disclosing Party understands the Recipient “may develop or have developed information internally, or receive or have received information from other parties that is similar to the Confidential Information.”

Additionally, Section 5.3 excludes confidentiality obligations for information that “is rightfully acquired from others who did not obtain it under obligation of confidentiality.”
