## Query

Consider NSK's Confidentiality Agreement for Suppliers; Does the document allow the Receiving Party to independently develop information that is similar to the Confidential Information?

## Gold

2.2. Confidential Information does not include information which:
d) which is independently developed by Receiving Party; or

## RAG Answer

Yes, effectively. Under clause 2.2(d), “Confidential Information” does **not** include information that is **independently developed by the Receiving Party**. Therefore, information that the Receiving Party develops independently—even if it is similar to the Confidential Information—would not be treated as Confidential Information and would not be subject to the confidentiality obligations.

However, the agreement does **not** expressly grant a right to develop similar information. It only excludes independently developed information from the definition of Confidential Information. The Receiving Party must still comply with clause 3.1(b), which prohibits using or exploiting the actual Confidential Information except for the agreed Purpose.
