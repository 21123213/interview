## Query

Consider The Munt's Non-Disclosure Agreement; Does the document allow the Receiving Party to share some Confidential Information with their employees?

## Gold

The Disclosee will procure that prior to the disclosure to any other person (including any professional advisor) of any Confidential Information, such other person is made aware of the provisions of this Agreement and the fact that the Disclosee will be liable.

## RAG Answer

Yes.

The NDA contemplates that Confidential Information may be provided to the Disclosee (the receiving party) and their “Representatives,” which expressly include responsible managers, officers, employees, shareholders, members of the Board of Directors, and advisors.

It also requires that before disclosing Confidential Information to any other person, including a professional advisor, the Disclosee must ensure that such person is made aware of the Agreement’s provisions and that the Disclosee remains liable for the disclosure.

Therefore, the document allows the receiving party to share Confidential Information with its employees, subject to those confidentiality and notification conditions.
