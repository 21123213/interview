## Query

Consider the Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement between AMC and Other Party; Does the document require that all Confidential Information be expressly identified by the Disclosing Party?

## Gold

(a) “Confidential Information” means any proprietary information that is disclosed in writing by Disclosing Party (defined herein) to Receiving Party (defined herein) and is duly and recognizably marked “Confidential” on each document / sheet.

## RAG Answer

No. The document does not require every item of Confidential Information to be expressly identified by the Disclosing Party.

Under Section 2(a), written Confidential Information must be “duly and recognizably marked ‘Confidential’” on each document/sheet, and documents/information/facts specifically listed in Exhibit A are deemed Confidential Information. However, Section 2(b) also covers information relating to the Disclosing Party’s business, technology, products, services, trade secrets, know-how, formulas, processes, ideas, and inventions that the Receiving Party “should reasonably understand” as confidential or proprietary. Therefore, express marking or identification is required for the primary written definition, but the agreement also protects information that need not be expressly identified if it should reasonably be understood as confidential.
