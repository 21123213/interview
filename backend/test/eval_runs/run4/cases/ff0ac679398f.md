## Query

Consider Media News Group, Inc.'s Non-Disclosure Agreement; Does the document allow the Receiving Party to acquire information similar to the Confidential Information from a third party?

## Gold

7. For purposes of this Agreement, “Confidential Information” shall not include any information which
(d) in the future is received from a third party who, to the knowledge of Recipient at the time of disclosure, is under no obligation of confidentiality with respect thereto.

## RAG Answer

Yes, but only under a limited condition.

Under Section 7(d) of the Media News Group, Inc. NDA, “Confidential Information” does **not** include information that the Recipient receives in the future from a third party who, to the Recipient’s knowledge at the time of disclosure, is **not under any obligation of confidentiality** with respect to that information.

So the document does not expressly prohibit the Receiving Party from obtaining similar information from a third party, but such third-party information is outside the NDA’s confidentiality restrictions only if the third party was not under a confidentiality obligation when it disclosed the information. If the third party was under a confidentiality obligation, the information may still be treated as Confidential Information and subject to the NDA’s restrictions.
