## Query

Consider tpi's Non-Disclosure Agreement; Does the document allow the Receiving Party to acquire information similar to the Confidential Information from a third party?

## Gold

Such Proprietary Information shall not include information which
(3) is disclosed to the Receiving Party by a third party who lawfully possesses such information and who is duly authorized or otherwise entitled to disclose such information;

## RAG Answer

Yes. Under TPI’s Non-Disclosure Agreement, information is not treated as “Proprietary Information” if it is disclosed to the Receiving Party by a third party who lawfully possesses it and is duly authorized or otherwise entitled to disclose it.

Therefore, the Receiving Party may acquire such information from a third party, provided the third party has lawful possession and authority to disclose it. If the information is merely “similar” but not derived from or equivalent to TPI’s Proprietary Information, the Agreement does not expressly prohibit that either.
