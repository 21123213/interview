## Query

Consider the Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement between Wayne Fueling Systems and Unknown Party; Does the document allow the Receiving Party to share some Confidential Information with third parties, including consultants, agents, and professional advisors?

## Gold

(iii) use the same degree of care as with its own confidential information, which shall be at least a reasonable standard of care, to prevent disclosure of the Confidential Information and Notes, except to its Affiliates, and its or their officers, directors, employees, agents, advisors, representatives, service providers, consultants and/or subcontractors (collectively, “Authorized Parties”), solely to the extent necessary to permit them to assist the Receiving Party in evaluating the Transaction; and

## RAG Answer

Yes. Under Section 2 of the Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement, the Receiving Party may disclose Confidential Information to its Affiliates and to its or their officers, directors, employees, agents, advisors, representatives, service providers, consultants, and/or subcontractors — collectively defined as “Authorized Parties” — but only to the extent necessary to permit them to assist the Receiving Party in evaluating the possible Transaction.

Before such disclosure, the Receiving Party must advise the Authorized Parties of the confidential nature of the Confidential Information, provide them with a copy of the Agreement, and direct them to abide by its terms. The Receiving Party remains responsible for any breach by it, its Affiliates, or its Authorized Parties.
