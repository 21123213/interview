## Query

Consider tpi's Non-Disclosure Agreement; Does the document allow the Receiving Party to share some Confidential Information with their employees?

## Gold

Such consent to disclose Proprietary Information to employees of Receiving Party or its affiliated company (“affiliated Receiving Party”) with a legitimate “need to know” and only for the purposes described in this Agreement is herewith given, but further consent shall be required for disclosure to others or authorization of use by others.

## RAG Answer

Yes. The TPI Non-Disclosure Agreement expressly permits disclosure of Proprietary/Confidential Information to employees of the Receiving Party or its affiliated Receiving Party, but only if they have a legitimate “need to know” and the disclosure is solely for the purposes described in the Agreement. The Receiving Party must also ensure that such recipients are bound by confidentiality and use restrictions consistent with the Agreement.
