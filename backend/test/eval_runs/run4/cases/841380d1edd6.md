## Query

Consider the Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement between Wayne Fueling Systems and Unknown Party; Does the document include a clause that prevents the Receiving Party from disclosing the fact that the Agreement was agreed upon or negotiated?

## Gold

## RAG Answer

Yes. Section 2(iv) of the Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement includes a clause restricting the Receiving Party from disclosing that the parties have had or are having discussions or negotiations with respect to the Transaction.

Specifically, the Receiving Party agrees, except as required by law, not to disclose to unauthorized persons:

> “that the Confidential Information has been made available, that the Receiving Party is considering a possible Transaction or that the parties have had or are having discussions or negotiations with respect thereto.”

This covers disclosure that the parties negotiated or were negotiating the Transaction. The provided text does not expressly state that the Receiving Party may not disclose the mere fact that the Agreement itself was signed or agreed upon, but it does prohibit disclosure of the fact that discussions or negotiations occurred.
