## Query

Consider NSK's Confidentiality Agreement for Suppliers; Does the document allow the Receiving Party to acquire information similar to the Confidential Information from a third party?

## Gold

2.2. Confidential Information does not include information which:
c) which is lawfully obtained by the Receiving Party from a third party and is not subject to a similar restriction on disclosure;

## RAG Answer

Yes, in effect. Clause 2.2(c) of the NSK Confidentiality Agreement for Suppliers states that “Confidential Information” does **not** include information that is **lawfully obtained by the Receiving Party from a third party and is not subject to a similar restriction on disclosure**.

Therefore, if the Receiving Party acquires similar information from a third party **lawfully** and the third-party information is **not subject to a similar confidentiality restriction**, it falls outside the definition of Confidential Information and is not restricted by the Agreement’s confidentiality obligations.
