## Query

Consider Aspiegel's Non-Disclosure Agreement; Does the document include a clause that prevents the Receiving Party from disclosing the fact that the Agreement was agreed upon or negotiated?

## Gold

Confidential Information: means all confidential information (however recorded, preserved or disclosed) disclosed by a Party or its Representatives to the other Party and that Party's Representatives including but not limited to:
(a) the fact that discussions and negotiations are taking place concerning the Purpose and the status of those discussions and negotiations;
(b) the existence and terms of this Agreement;

## RAG Answer

Yes.

The Aspiegel NDA template treats the fact of negotiations and the existence of the Agreement as Confidential Information:

- Clause 1 defines “Confidential Information” to include **“the fact that discussions and negotiations are taking place concerning the Purpose and the status of those discussions and negotiations”**.
- It also includes **“the existence and terms of this Agreement.”**
- Clause 2 then requires the Recipient Party to keep all Confidential Information secret and confidential, and not to disclose it to any third party without the Disclosing Party’s written consent, except as permitted under the Agreement.

So the document does include a clause preventing the Receiving Party from disclosing that the Agreement was agreed upon or that negotiations were taking place, subject to the limited exceptions in Clause 4.
