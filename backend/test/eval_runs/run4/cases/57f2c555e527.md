## Query

Consider the Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement between Bosch and Automotive Service Solutions; Does the document require that all Confidential Information be expressly identified by the Disclosing Party?

## Gold

The term “Confidential Information” as used herein means all nonpublic information relating to the Subject Matter that is disclosed by either party, its Affiliates (as defined below), or their agents (where applicable, collectively referred to as the “Disclosing Party”), directly or indirectly, in writing, orally or by inspection of premises or tangible objects to the other party (the “Recipient”) that is: (i) marked confidential or proprietary, or (ii) given the nature of the information or the circumstances surrounding its disclosure, reasonably should be deemed confidential.

## RAG Answer

No. The Agreement does not require that all Confidential Information be expressly identified or marked by the Disclosing Party.

Under Section 1, “Confidential Information” includes information that is either:

1. marked confidential or proprietary; or  
2. given the nature of the information or the circumstances surrounding its disclosure, reasonably should be deemed confidential.

Therefore, information may be treated as Confidential Information even if it is not expressly marked or identified as confidential.
