## Query

Consider AfriGIS's Non-Disclosure Agreement; Does the document allow the Receiving Party to independently develop information that is similar to the Confidential Information?

## Gold

The obligations of the Receiving Party pursuant to the provisions of this Agreement shall not apply to any Confidential Information that –
8.3 is developed independently of the Disclosing Party by the Receiving Party in circumstances that do not amount to a breach of the provisions of this Agreement;

## RAG Answer

Yes, in effect—but only under a limited exclusion.

Under clause 8.3 of AfriGIS’s NDA, the Receiving Party’s confidentiality obligations do **not** apply to information that:

> “is developed independently of the Disclosing Party by the Receiving Party in circumstances that do not amount to a breach of the provisions of this Agreement.”

So, if the Receiving Party independently develops information that is similar to the Confidential Information, and it does so **without using the Confidential Information or otherwise breaching the NDA**, the NDA treats that independently developed information as excluded Confidential Information.

However, the document does not give a general right to copy, use, or disclose similar information merely because it resembles Confidential Information. The key condition is genuine independent development without breach.
