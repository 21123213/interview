## Query

Consider the Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement between Wayne Fueling Systems and Unknown Party; Does the document require that all Confidential Information be expressly identified by the Disclosing Party?

## Gold

Confidential Information also includes, but is not limited to, personal data as defined in this Agreement or by applicable law, whichever is broader, and personal data shall not be required to be marked “Confidential” or “Proprietary” to be treated as Confidential Information under this Agreement.

## RAG Answer

No. The Agreement generally requires Confidential Information to be identified/marked, but not all Confidential Information must be expressly marked by the Disclosing Party.

Under Section 1, Confidential Information is limited to information disclosed by the Disclosing Party or its Affiliates that is:

1. In writing, email, or other tangible electronic storage medium and clearly marked “Confidential” or “Proprietary”; or  
2. Disclosed orally or visually, and then followed within ten working days by a written disclosure marked “Confidential” or “Proprietary.”

However, the Agreement also expressly includes “personal data” as Confidential Information, and personal data does not need to be marked “Confidential” or “Proprietary” to be protected. All other information is deemed non-confidential.
