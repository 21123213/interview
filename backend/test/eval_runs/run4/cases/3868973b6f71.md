## Query

Consider The Munt's Non-Disclosure Agreement; Does the document allow the Receiving Party to share some Confidential Information with third parties, including consultants, agents, and professional advisors?

## Gold

The Disclosee will procure that prior to the disclosure to any other person (including any professional advisor) of any Confidential Information, such other person is made aware of the provisions of this Agreement and the fact that the Disclosee will be liable.

## RAG Answer

Yes, but only in a limited and conditional way.

The NDA does not permit unrestricted sharing with third parties. However, it allows the Disclosee/Candidate to disclose Confidential Information to other persons, including professional advisors, provided that **before disclosure** the person receiving the information is made aware of the Agreement’s provisions and that the Disclosee will be liable.

The document also refers to “Representatives,” which include advisors such as financial, legal, tax advisors and auditors. Therefore, sharing with consultants, agents, or professional advisors may be permitted only if they are bound by the confidentiality requirements and the Disclosee remains responsible for any breach.
