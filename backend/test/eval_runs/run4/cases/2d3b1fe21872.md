## Query

Consider the Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement between AMC and Other Party; Does the document allow the Receiving Party to acquire information similar to the Confidential Information from a third party?

## Gold

(c) Confidential Information does not include any information that Receiving Party can document:
(iv) is rightfully obtained by Receiving Party from a third party without any obligation of confidentiality to Disclosing Party.

## RAG Answer

Yes, but only under a specific condition.

Section 2(c)(iv) of the Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement states that “Confidential Information” does **not** include information that the Receiving Party can **document** was **rightfully obtained from a third party** and that the third party obtained or disclosed it **without any obligation of confidentiality to the Disclosing Party**.

So, if the Receiving Party can document that information similar to or corresponding to the Confidential Information was rightfully obtained from a third party that had no confidentiality obligation to the Disclosing Party, then that information is excluded from the definition of Confidential Information.

However, the document does not simply allow acquisition of “similar” information in general. The key requirements are:

1. The Receiving Party must be able to **document** the third-party source; and  
2. The third-party information must have been obtained **rightfully** and **without any confidentiality obligation to the Disclosing Party**.
