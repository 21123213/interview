## RAG Answer

Yes. The AGProjects Non-Disclosure Agreement restricts the use of Confidential Information to the stated purpose.

Specifically, Section 2 (“Limited Purpose”) states that the Receiving Party may use the Confidential Information **solely for the purpose of discussing AG Projects’ services and products**.

The Agreement also reinforces this restriction in Section 7 (“No Use”), which prohibits the Receiving Party from selling, licensing, or otherwise exploiting any products, services, documents, or information that embody Confidential Information, and clarifies that providing Confidential Information does not grant any license or other rights.
