## Query

Consider the Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement between Inventor and Product Development Experts, Inc.; Does the document permit the Receiving Party to create a copy of some Confidential Information under certain circumstances?

## Gold

If any party makes copies of the Confidential Information of the other party, such copies shall also constitute Confidential Information and any and all confidential markings on such documents shall be maintained.

## RAG Answer

Yes, but only in a limited and implied sense. The Agreement permits each party to use the other party’s Confidential Information only to evaluate and discuss a potential business relationship. It also states that if a party makes copies of Confidential Information, those copies remain Confidential Information and must retain any confidential markings. Therefore, a Receiving Party may create a copy only as needed for that permitted evaluation/discussion purpose and subject to the confidentiality obligations; the Agreement does not grant a general right to copy.
