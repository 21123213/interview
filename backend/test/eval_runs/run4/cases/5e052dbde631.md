## Query

Consider the Non-Disclosure Agreement between CEII and NDA; Does the document permit the Receiving Party to create a copy of some Confidential Information under certain circumstances?

## Gold

The Receiving Party may make copies of CEII, but such copies become CEII and subject to these same terms and conditions.
The Receiving Party shall provide attested certification from an authorized representative confirming such return and destruction "provided however, Receiving Party may retain one (1) copy of such documentation in its secure legal files for the sole purpose of administering its obligations under this agreement, as well as copies of electronically exchanged Confidential Information that are made as a matter of routine information technology back-up, which copies shall continue to be kept confidential in accordance with the terms and conditions of this Agreement."

## RAG Answer

Yes. The document expressly permits the Receiving Party to make copies of CEII, which is defined as Confidential Information, provided that:

- “The Receiving Party may make copies of CEII, but such copies become CEII and subject to these same terms and conditions.”
- The Receiving Party must still maintain confidentiality and comply with the Agreement.

It also allows limited retention of copies after return/destruction:

- One copy may be retained in secure legal files solely to administer the Agreement.
- Routine IT backup copies may be retained, but must remain confidential.
