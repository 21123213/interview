## Query

Consider the Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement between Boston Consulting Group and Unknown Party; Does the document allow the Receiving Party to acquire information similar to the Confidential Information from a third party?

## Gold

The following types of information, however marked, are not confidential information. Information that:
 Is received from another source who can disclose it lawfully and without an obligation to keep it confidential;

## RAG Answer

Yes, under the uploaded Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement, the Receiving Party may acquire information similar to the other party’s Confidential Information from a third party, provided the third party can lawfully disclose it and has no obligation to keep it confidential.

The agreement excludes from “Confidential Information” any information that:

- “Is received from another source who can disclose it lawfully and without an obligation to keep it confidential”;
- “Was lawfully known to the receiver of the information without an obligation to keep it confidential”; or
- “Is independently developed.”

Therefore, if the similar information comes from such a lawful third-party source or is independently developed, it is not treated as Confidential Information under the agreement.

Note: the document identifies “BCG” as **The Bleecker Consulting Group, LLC**, not Boston Consulting Group.
