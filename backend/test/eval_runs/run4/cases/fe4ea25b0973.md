## Query

Consider the Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement between AMC and Other Party; Does the document permit the Receiving Party to retain some Confidential Information even after its return or destruction?

## Gold

Nothing contained herein shall require the destruction or purging of Confidential Information maintained on routine computer system backup tapes, disks or similar storage devices.

## RAG Answer

Yes. Under Section 4(b), the Receiving Party must return or certify destruction of all copies or extracts of Confidential Information upon request, but the agreement expressly states that nothing requires the destruction or purging of Confidential Information maintained on routine computer system backup tapes, disks, or similar storage devices. Thus, backup-retained Confidential Information may be retained.
