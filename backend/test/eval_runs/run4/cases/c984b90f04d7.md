## Query

Consider the Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement between Wayne Fueling Systems and Unknown Party; Does the document allow the Receiving Party to acquire information similar to the Confidential Information from a third party?

## Gold

3. This Agreement shall be inoperative as to particular portions of the Confidential Information disclosed by the Disclosing Party if such information:
(iii) is or becomes available to the Receiving Party, its Affiliates, or its or their Authorized Parties on a non-confidential basis from a source other than the Disclosing Party when such source is not, to the best of the Receiving Party’s knowledge, subject to a confidentiality obligation with the Disclosing Party; or

## RAG Answer

Yes. The document permits the Receiving Party to acquire information similar to the Confidential Information from a third party, provided it is obtained lawfully and outside the confidentiality restrictions.

Specifically, Section 3 states that the Agreement is “inoperative” as to portions of Confidential Information that:

> “is or becomes available to the Receiving Party, its Affiliates, or its or their Authorized Parties on a non-confidential basis from a source other than the Disclosing Party when such source is not, to the best of the Receiving Party’s knowledge, subject to a confidentiality obligation with the Disclosing Party.”

So if the Receiving Party receives similar information from a third party on a non-confidential basis, and that third party is not known to be subject to a confidentiality obligation with the Disclosing Party, the NDA does not restrict the Receiving Party from using or possessing that information.

Section 7 also supports this by stating that the Agreement does not restrict either party’s right to “undertake similar discussions or to enter into agreements or relationships with third parties covering subjects related to the matters covered herein.”

However, this permission does not allow the Receiving Party to use or disclose the actual Confidential Information received from the Disclosing Party for purposes outside the Transaction.
