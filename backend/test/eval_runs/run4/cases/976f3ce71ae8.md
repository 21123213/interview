## Query

Consider the Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement between Wayne Fueling Systems and Unknown Party; Does the document allow verbally conveyed information to be considered as Confidential Information?

## Gold

“Confidential Information” as used in this agreement (the “Agreement”) shall mean all such information that is or has been disclosed by the Disclosing Party or its Affiliates (defined below):
(ii) orally or visually, and then followed within ten (10) working days thereafter with a disclosure complying with the requirements of clause (i) above.

## RAG Answer

Yes, but only conditionally. Under Section 1 of the Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement, information disclosed **orally or visually** can be treated as **Confidential Information** only if it is followed **within ten (10) working days** by a disclosure that satisfies the written/electronic marking requirement—i.e., disclosed in writing, by email, or other tangible electronic storage medium and clearly marked **“Confidential”** or **“Proprietary.”**

So, verbally conveyed information is not automatically Confidential Information unless it is later confirmed in a compliant marked written/electronic disclosure within the required period.
