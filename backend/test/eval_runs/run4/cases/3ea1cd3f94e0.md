## Query

Consider tpi's Non-Disclosure Agreement; Does the document permit the Receiving Party to retain some Confidential Information even after its return or destruction?

## Gold

4. Immediately upon a request by the Disclosing Party at any time, the Receiving Party will turn over to the Disclosing Party all Proprietary Information of the Disclosing Party and all documents or media containing any such Proprietary Information and any and all copies or extracts thereof or upon request of the Disclosing Party, the Receiving Party shall certify in writing that all materials containing Proprietary Information (including all copies thereof) have been destroyed.

## RAG Answer

No. TPI’s Non-Disclosure Agreement does not appear to permit the Receiving Party to retain Confidential/Proprietary Information after return or destruction.

Under Paragraph 4, upon the Disclosing Party’s request, the Receiving Party must turn over **all Proprietary Information**, all documents or media containing it, and **any and all copies or extracts**, or certify in writing that all materials containing Proprietary Information, **including all copies**, have been destroyed. The provided document contains no exception allowing the Receiving Party to keep any copies or retained materials after such return or destruction.
