## Query

Consider the Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement between AMC and Other Party; Does the document specify whether the Receiving Party is required to destroy or return Confidential Information upon the termination of the Agreement?

## Gold

At expiration Receiving Party must cease all use of and upon request from Disclosing Party return to Disclosing Party all copies or extracts of Disclosing Party’s Confidential Information, in any medium, or certify, in writing by an authorized officer of Receiving Party, the destruction of the same to Disclosing Party.

## RAG Answer

Yes. Section 4(b) specifies that, at expiration of the Agreement, the Receiving Party must cease all use of the Confidential Information and, upon request from the Disclosing Party, either return all copies or extracts of the Confidential Information or certify in writing that they have been destroyed.

However, this return/destruction obligation is triggered by the Disclosing Party’s request, and the Agreement expressly does not require destruction or purging of Confidential Information maintained on routine computer system backup tapes, disks, or similar storage devices.
