## Query

Consider the Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement between AMC and Other Party; Does the document permit the Receiving Party to create a copy of some Confidential Information under certain circumstances?

## Gold

Receiving Party must not copy, alter, modify, reverse engineer, or attempt to derive the composition or underlying information, structure or ideas of any Confidential Information and must not remove, overprint, deface or change any notice of confidentiality, copyright, trademark, logo, legend or other notices of ownership from any originals or copies of Confidential Information it receives from the Disclosing Party.

## RAG Answer

No. The provided Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement states in Section 4(a) that the Receiving Party “must not copy” any Confidential Information, and it does not include an express exception permitting the Receiving Party to create copies under certain circumstances.

The only related provision is Section 4(b), which says that nothing requires the destruction or purging of Confidential Information maintained on routine computer system backup tapes, disks, or similar storage devices. However, that is a limited retention/backup exception, not permission to create new copies of Confidential Information.
