## Query

Consider VELCO's Non-Disclosure Agreement; Does the document require that all Confidential Information be expressly identified by the Disclosing Party?

## Gold

For purposes of this agreement, CI shall mean any confidential, proprietary or trade secret information that is owned or controlled, or is specifically marked or identified as “Confidential”, by VELCO prior to disclosure to Recipient.

## RAG Answer

No—not uniformly.

Under the VELCO NDA, “Classified Information” includes CEII, BCSI, and business Confidential Information (CI):

- For **CI**, the document defines it as information “owned or controlled” by VELCO **or** “specifically marked or identified as ‘Confidential’” by VELCO prior to disclosure. So express marking/identification is not always required for CI.
- For **CEII** and **BCSI**, the document requires that VELCO **designate** the information as such, and written CEII/BCSI must be labeled “Confidential CEII” or “Confidential BCSI.”

So the document does **not** require all Confidential Information to be expressly identified by the Disclosing Party; it imposes express designation/labeling requirements mainly for CEII and BCSI, while CI may be covered if owned/controlled by VELCO even if not expressly marked.
